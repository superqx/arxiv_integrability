## High Energy Theory

|---|---|---|---|---|
|**2026-08-24**|**Multiparameter Quantum Affine Spaces and the Scalene Yang--Baxter Equation**|Pramod Padmanabhan, Somnath Maity, Vladimir Korepin|[[arxiv:2608.20714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20714)]|This paper introduces a new method for constructing solutions to the non-braided scalene Yang-Baxter equation, a variant of the standard equation that does not require braiding. The authors build these solutions from quadratic noncommutative algebras, starting with two anticommuting generators and extending the approach to any number of generators, where the relation enforces a specific multiplicative form for the exchange matrix. They provide explicit finite- and infinite-dimensional realizations using singular matrices, Heisenberg-Weyl operators, and weighted shifts. ## Condensed Matter

|Publish Date|Title|Authors|arXiv|Summary|

|**2026-08-24**|**Two distinct gap structures in the mid-infrared optical conductivity of the Hubbard model**|Dongwook Kim, Karsten Held|[[arxiv:2608.21284](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21284)]|This paper investigates the origin of universal mid-infrared (MIR) optical conductivity features in cuprate superconductors. Using the Hubbard model, the authors identify two distinct gaps—a doping-dependent pseudogap from antiferromagnetic spin fluctuations and a Mott-Hubbard gap—that produce separable, characteristic spectral shapes. Their numerical and analytical results show that the doping dependence of both gaps matches experimental data, offering a unified microscopic explanation for the MIR response. The work clarifies how Mott correlations and spin fluctuations jointly shape cuprate optical spectra.|
|**2026-08-24**|**Doping-driven Mott transition from steady-state density functional theory**|David Jacob, Stefan Kurth|[[arxiv:2608.21029](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21029)]|This paper presents a density functional theory framework, specifically steady-state i-DFT, to describe the doping-driven Mott transition in the Hubbard model. To capture the many-body spectral function, the authors derive an approximation for the exchange-correlation bias by linking it to the quasiparticle weight via Fermi-liquid theory. Their numerical results show that this functional successfully reproduces the doping-driven Mott transition. The work establishes i-DFT as a viable method for studying correlated electron systems out of equilibrium.|
|**2026-08-24**|**Multiparameter Quantum Affine Spaces and the Scalene Yang--Baxter Equation**|Pramod Padmanabhan, Somnath Maity, Vladimir Korepin|[[arxiv:2608.20714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20714)]|This paper introduces a new method for constructing solutions to the non-braided scalene Yang-Baxter equation using quadratic noncommutative algebras. Starting with two anticommuting generators, the authors derive a continuous family of solutions, which they then extend to systems with any number of generators, where the equation uniquely fixes the exchange matrix to a multiplicative form. They provide both finite- and infinite-dimensional matrix realizations of these solutions using tools like singular matrices, Heisenberg-Weyl operators, and weighted shifts. The key finding is that these solutions are "purely scalene," meaning they solve the equation without their individual operators satisfying the standard Yang-Baxter equation, establishing a new algebraic framework for studying such irreducible triples.|
|**2026-08-24**|**Graphon Spin Systems as Exactly Solvable Models**|Artem Alexandrov, Georgi S. Medvedev|[[arxiv:2608.20410](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20410)]|This paper introduces a framework for analyzing the Ising model on large networks by using graphons, which describe convergent graph sequences and encompass many common topologies. The authors derive the mean-field limit of the model and show that critical temperatures are determined by the eigenvalues of the graphon’s associated Hilbert-Schmidt operator. They apply this to Erdős–Rényi, small-world, and power-law networks, revealing ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic transitions, plus multistability in the small-world case, which they verify with Monte Carlo simulations. The work bridges exactly solvable mean-field models and diverse network structures, offering a powerful analytical tool for studying phase transitions in complex systems.|
|**2026-08-24**|**Quasiparticle Diffusion for the Toda Fluid in Equilibrium**|Seema Chahal, Alberto Brollo, Indranil Mukherjee, Abhishek Dhar, Anupam Kundu, Herbert Spohn|[[arxiv:2606.15131](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15131)]|This paper introduces a numerical method to track quasiparticle trajectories in the Toda fluid, an integrable many-body system, by using the time-dependent eigenvectors of its Lax matrix. The method is applied to equilibrium simulations, where it confirms that individual quasiparticles undergo Brownian motion and that tagged particles also diffuse. The measured diffusion constant agrees with a new prediction from thermodynamic Bethe ansatz (TBA) theory. The approach is significant because it provides a general tool for studying quasiparticle dynamics in other classical integrable models with a Lax matrix, going beyond the previously tractable hard-rods case.|
|**2026-08-24**|**Explicit proof of Anderson's orthogonality catastrophe for the one-dimensional Fermi polaron with attractive interaction**|Giuliano Orso|[[arxiv:2604.12475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12475)]|This paper presents a fully analytical proof of Anderson's orthogonality catastrophe in an exactly solvable one-dimensional Fermi polaron model with attractive interactions. The method combines determinant representations for wavefunction overlaps, derived from the Bethe ansatz, with properties of Cauchy matrices to calculate the thermodynamic limit. The authors show that the quasi-particle residue decays algebraically with particle number and confirm the Anderson exponent matches the Bethe-ansatz phase shift. They also derive the strong-coupling behavior of the amplitude prefactor, providing a complete, rigorous verification of the phenomenon in this integrable system.|
