|**2026-07-31**|**Quantum Trigonometric Spin Ruijsenaars-Schneider Models from $K$ -theoretic Coulomb Branches**|Gleb Arutyunov, Lukas Hardi, Rob Klabbers|[[arxiv:2607.28043](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28043)]|The paper addresses the quantization of a trigonometric spin Ruijsenaars-Schneider model, a many-body integrable system with N particles and ℓ internal spin states. The method leverages a description of the classical model based on the K-theoretic Coulomb branch of a 4D N=2 quiver gauge theory, using an algebra of L-operators from abelianized monopole operators to construct commuting Hamiltonians. Key results include showing that the lowest Hamiltonian matches a quantum determinant of a horizontal quantum loop algebra within the Coulomb branch algebra, and deriving commutation relations and equations of motion for the quantized spin variables. This work provides a rigorous algebraic framework for quantizing these models, linking them to integrable spin chains and Bethe subalgebras, which is significant for advancing the understanding of integrable systems in gauge theory.|
|**2026-07-31**|**On weight modules over truncated shifted iYangians**|Yaolong Shen, Linliang Song, Rui Xiong|[[arxiv:2607.27596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27596)]|This paper addresses the representation theory of truncated shifted iYangians, algebras linked to the quantization of affine Grassmannian slices. The authors introduce a new family of algebras called orientifold KLRW (oKLRW) algebras and establish their key properties, including polynomial representations and diagrammatic bases. They further construct diagrammatic realizations of homomorphisms connecting these algebras to KLR iYangians. The main result is an equivalence between integral weight modules of truncated shifted iYangians and nilpotent modules of interval oKLRW algebras, providing a new algebraic framework for studying these modules.|
|**2026-07-31**|**Symmetry-driven thermalization via finite de Finetti theorems**|Uttam Singh, Nicolas J. Cerf|[[arxiv:2604.09410](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09410)]|The paper proposes a deterministic alternative to statistical mechanisms for thermalization in closed quantum systems. The authors prove a finite de Finetti-type theorem showing that quantum states invariant under energy-preserving unitaries have reduced subsystems that are close to mixtures of thermal product states, with errors vanishing as system size grows. They also demonstrate a Lindblad dynamics example whose steady state falls into this symmetry class. This establishes that symmetry alone can enforce thermal behavior, offering a new fundamental principle beyond chaos or ergodicity.|
|**2026-07-31**|**Descriptive power and predictive limits of a discrete Hasimoto--DNLS model of protein backbone structure**|Yiquan Wang|[[arxiv:2602.13160](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13160)]|This paper investigates whether a discrete nonlinear Schrödinger equation (DNLS), derived from the discrete Frenet geometry of protein backbones, can predict 3D structure from sequence. The authors decompose the DNLS effective potential into real and imaginary parts and find that the imaginary part encodes chirality, while the real part is almost entirely determined by local geometry, leaving minimal sequence dependence. They show that self-consistent field iterations fail to recover native structures, concluding that the Hasimoto map is a kinematic identity rather than a dynamical governing equation. The significance lies in clarifying that barriers to *ab initio* prediction stem from the local, real-potential reduction, not the lossless map itself.|
|**2026-07-31**|**Constrained Least Action and Quantum Mechanics**|Winfried Lohmiller, Jean-Jacques Slotine|[[arxiv:2401.03464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03464)]|This paper introduces a new method for solving the Schrödinger equation using only classical mechanics, specifically by computing the action from the Hamilton-Jacobi equation and deriving the wave function from classical quantities. The method requires a condition on the Laplacian of the action, which can be satisfied for nonlinear potentials through a time rescaling that corresponds to a change of variables. The authors demonstrate the approach by reproducing the known hydrogen atom wave functions and then computing new, previously unsolved quantum waves for a quartic oscillator. This work offers an analytical alternative to perturbation theory, extending quantum solutions to systems with nonlinear potentials or position-dependent inertia.|

|**2026-07-31**|**Finite-size effects and interaction-driven crossovers in quarter-filled attractive Hubbard model: Exact diagonalization, DMRG and machine-learning analysis**|Md Fahad Equbal, Satoru Hayami|[[arxiv:2607.27916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27916)]|This paper studies the quarter-filled attractive Hubbard model on finite-width cylindrical lattices, focusing on how pairing evolves as interaction strength increases. Using exact diagonalization, DMRG, and unsupervised machine-learning techniques (PCA and UMAP), the authors identify a continuous crossover from weakly correlated fermions to tightly bound singlet pairs, consistent with BCS–BEC crossover behavior. Key results show that two-hole binding is always present in the attractive regime, while three-hole binding only emerges at strong attraction with notable finite-size effects, and machine-learning methods independently detect the same crossover region without relying on predefined order parameters. The findings provide robust benchmarks for quantum-simulation experiments and demonstrate the value of combining conventional many-body approaches with unsupervised learning for analyzing correlated quantum systems.|
|**2026-07-31**|**Universality of Energy-Space Entanglement in Quantum Impurity Models**|Geng-Dong Zhou, Zhi-Da Song|[[arxiv:2607.27394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27394)]|This paper introduces an energy-space bipartition for studying entanglement in quantum impurity models, complementing the usual real-space approach. By partitioning the bath into high- and low-energy sectors, the authors find that the low-energy entanglement entropy flows to universal constants at Fermi-liquid fixed points, independent of model parameters. These constants are integer multiples of ln 2, which can be understood through a mapping to a one-dimensional chain and classified via topological band theory, with each contribution originating from a topological edge mode. The work also examines transitions between fixed points and non-Fermi-liquid critical points, demonstrating the universality of this energy-space entanglement and providing a new tool for classifying quantum impurity systems.|
|**2026-07-31**|**Thermal Activation of Divergent Distillable Entanglement under Non-Abelian Strong Symmetry**|Shuai Zeng|[[arxiv:2607.12710](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12710)]|This paper demonstrates that, contrary to typical intuition, heating can generate a macroscopic amount of distillable entanglement in quantum systems with non-Abelian strong symmetries. The authors analyze an exactly solvable dimer chain where entanglement is zero at zero temperature but becomes proportional to half the logarithm of the system size at any finite temperature, achieved by measuring representation labels to convert thermal fluctuations into standard ebits. They generalize this result to broader classes of SU(2)-invariant chains and show the entanglement yield is bounded below by a diverging term at high temperatures, with a Bessel-function crossover governing finite-size behavior. The findings establish that thermalization can convert non-Abelian symmetry constraints into a practically unbounded operational quantum resource, fundamentally reshaping our understanding of entanglement under heating.|
|**2026-07-31**|**Exact Fractionalized Ground States in an Extended Spin-1 Kitaev Chain**|Alwyn Jose Raja, R. Ganesh|[[arxiv:2510.12880](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12880)]|This paper presents exact solutions for a spin-1 chain with extended Kitaev-like couplings, inspired by the AKLT model. The method involves identifying an exactly solvable point where the Hamiltonian is a sum of projection operators onto spin components along bond directions, enabling the construction of ground states as matrix product states. Key results include an exponential ground state degeneracy involving fractionalized spin-1/2 objects and a phase diagram tuned by the ratio of bilinear to biquadratic terms. The significance lies in providing an exact reference point for understanding the spin-1 Kitaev model and offering variational approximations for its ground state and low-energy excitations.|

|**2026-07-31**|**Descriptive power and predictive limits of a discrete Hasimoto--DNLS model of protein backbone structure**|Yiquan Wang|[[arxiv:2602.13160](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13160)]|This paper re-examines a discrete Hasimoto transform that maps protein backbones to a complex field obeying a discrete nonlinear Schrödinger equation (DNLS), asking whether this mapping can predict structure. By decomposing the DNLS potential into real and imaginary parts with machine-precision accuracy across hundreds of proteins, the authors uncover key limitations: the imaginary part's chirality creates a massive degeneracy, and sequence dependence contributes less than 5% to the potential. Self-consistent iterations fail to recover native structures, yet the DNLS dispersion residual effectively identifies alpha-helices. The central conclusion is that the transform is a kinematic identity, not a dynamical folding equation, with predictive barriers arising from local, real-potential reductions rather than the lossless mapping itself.|
