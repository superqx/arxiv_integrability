|**2026-10-26**|**Chaotic behaviors of particles around the black hole with an anisotropic matter immersed in a magnetic field**|Khusan Alibekov, Hocheol Lee, Pakhlavon Yovkochev, Bobomurat J. |**2026-10-26**|**Classical elliptic ${\rm BC}_1$ Ruijsenaars-van Diejen model: relation to Zhukovsky-Volterra gyrostat and 1-site classical XYZ model with boundaries**|A. Mostovskii, A. Using Chalykh's L-operator, the authors factorize it into two Lax matrices, apply an IRF-Vertex gauge transformation, and express the result as a product of Lax matrices for Zhukovsky–Volterra gyrostats, each governed by a BC₁ classical Sklyanin algebra. These results establish explicit links between Ruijsenaars–van Diejen systems, gyrostats, and boundary XYZ spin chains, clarifying their shared algebraic origin.|

The authors show that both quasiparticles can be obtained from the same Bethe-Salpeter formalism by solving the full equation beyond the Tamm-Dancoff approximation, applied to model Hamiltonians. Using ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic graphene described by the extended Hubbard model, they clarify how on-site versus non-local interactions govern exciton and magnon formation and identify consistency requirements for extending the method to first-principles calculations. |**2026-10-26**|**Hybrid Monte Carlo for the extended Kane-Mele-Hubbard model**|Finn L. The authors develop continuous auxiliary field formulations amenable to the Hybrid Monte Carlo (HMC) algorithm and use them to map the sign problem across the model's parameter space. They identify the regions where HMC simulations are feasible, providing a first step toward determining the full phase diagram. The authors introduce an algorithmic advancement that simultaneously stabilizes both the determinant and force computations to machine precision while maintaining the scaling of standard determinant Quantum Monte Carlo. |**2026-10-26**|**Importance of effective Coulomb interactions for $T_c$ in cuprates**|Jak\v{s}a Vu\v{c}i\v{c}evi\'c, Upendra Kumar, Chia-Nan Yeh, Miguel A.
