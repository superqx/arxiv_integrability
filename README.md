The authors prove that the Reshetikhin condition is both necessary and sufficient for Yang-Baxter integrability in a broad standard setting, effectively linking this hidden algebraic structure to a simple local conservation law. This criterion is equivalent to the conservation of the total energy current, making it experimentally accessible and providing a quantum analogue to the Liouville-Arnold theorem. The finding simplifies the search for integrable spin chains by offering a direct test on local Hamiltonians instead of requiring the construction of R-matrices.|
|**2026-08-03**|**Chiral symmetry and curvature bounds in de Sitter spacetime**|L. G. By applying exponential proper-time regularization, the authors derive an exact solution for the constituent fermion mass in the strong-curvature limit, expressed using the Lambert W function. They find that the requirement for a real-valued mass solution imposes an upper bound on the cosmological constant, which is interpreted as a limitation of the mean-field approximation rather than a fundamental constraint on spacetime. The work provides a new analytical handle on fermion mass generation in curved spacetime and clarifies the role of curvature in restricting effective field theory descriptions.|

The authors achieve this by using monopole operators in the GKLO representation, which also reveals the underlying affine Yangian and quantum toroidal superintegrability. They further conjecture that extending this framework to elliptic Coulomb branches will yield the elliptic spin RS model. This work establishes a concrete link between gauge theory and integrable many-body systems, offering a new mathematical framework for studying RS models and their generalizations.|

The solutions found include nonlinear Meissner states with universal tails, vortex sheets, and periodic laminar structures, with the thermodynamic critical field emerging as a marginal profile. The vortex sheet is characterized as a soliton representing the coarse-grained limit of a dense vortex row, while the periodic solutions correspond to laminar states akin to rectangular vortex lattices. The work provides an exact framework for understanding the nonlinear behavior and intermediate structures in extreme type-II superconducting materials.|
|**2026-08-03**|**Haantjes torsion and integrability: a proof of Bolsinov-Konyaev-Matveev's conjecture**|Alessandro Arsie, Paolo Lorenzoni|[[arxiv:2607.29373](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29373)]|This paper proves a conjecture by Bolsinov, Konyaev, and Matveev regarding the integrability of hydrodynamic-type systems. The authors show that if a system defined by a \(\mathfrak{gl}\)-regular operator matrix \(A\) is integrable at a point, then the Haantjes tensor of \(A\) and all its symmetries must vanish in a neighborhood of that point. Consequently, they establish that such integrable systems can be locally expressed in a simplified form involving a commutative associative product with Hertling-Manin conditions. This result provides a rigorous geometric characterization of integrability for a broad class of hydrodynamic systems, linking algebraic and differential-geometric properties.|

They obtain analytic expressions across various coupling regimes and near quantum phase transitions, and validate their theory by simulating two experimental protocols. The predicted Drude weights show excellent agreement with recent experimental measurements, providing a direct bridge between transport theory and ultracold atom experiments.|

They derive a new action-angle description that explains the convergence to a generalized Gibbs ensemble while also accounting for the persistent oscillations linked to a high-energy mode. The work shows that standard thermal universality is recovered in the appropriate scaling limit, clarifying the boundaries between integrable and thermalizing dynamics.|
