|**2026-11-20**|**Analytic and Numerical Investigation of a Proposed Non-linear Extension of Quantum Mechanics**|Alan Chodos, Anastasiia Purtova|[[arxiv:2611.25188](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25188)]|The paper investigates additional properties of a previously proposed nonlinear extension of quantum mechanics, focusing on its behavior under a periodic driving force. Analytically, the authors identify a limit in which the model reduces to standard linear quantum mechanics and perform a perturbation analysis around a known exact solution, using Floquet theory to characterize the system. Numerically, they compare the exact solution with the analytic approximation and illustrate how nonlinear effects alter particle trajectories, especially near the linear limit. These results clarify the connection between the nonlinear model and ordinary quantum mechanics and demonstrate the practical impact of the nonlinearities on dynamics.|
|**2026-11-20**|**Exact ultrastatic multi-throat wormholes: Geometry, energy conditions, and optical cavities**|Francisco S. N. Lobo, Manuel E. Rodrigues, Gabriel I. R\'ois|[[arxiv:2611.24965](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24965)]|The paper addresses the lack of exactly solvable wormhole models with multiple throats by constructing a static, spherically symmetric hierarchy in which an order-N geometry contains N+1 throats and N anti-throats arranged as an alternating chain of necks and interior cavities. The factorized areal-radius prescription keeps the metric elementary and asymptotically flat, and the authors derive universal ultrastatic results from the Einstein equations: throats violate the radial null energy condition and host unstable light rings, while anti-throats satisfy all standard energy conditions and host stable light rings, with every finite cell carrying negative integrated radial null energy. The first nontrivial member interpolates continuously from the Ellis-Bronnikov wormhole through a degenerate throat to a double-throat geometry and admits closed expressions for its source, embedding, and traversability, with vanishing ADM mass arising from the hierarchy's radial falloff. These exact multi-cavity optical chains are significant because they provide analytically tractable laboratories for studying energy-condition violations, light-ring stability, and averaged exoticity in multiply connected spacetimes.|
|**2026-11-20**|**Kerr-Newman and genuine Kerr black holes in the Bertotti-Robinson magnetic field**|Hryhorii Ovcharenko, Jiri Podolsky|[[arxiv:2608.21672](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21672)]|The paper constructs an exact solution of the Einstein-Maxwell equations describing a rotating, charged black hole immersed in an external magnetic (or electric) field aligned with the rotation axis, characterized by mass, charge, Kerr-like rotation, and field strength. The solution has a simple closed form and reduces to the Kerr-Newman metric when the field vanishes and to the Bertotti-Robinson universe for zero mass and charge. The authors show that a recently published Kerr-Bertotti-Robinson solution corresponds to a special charge case and identify a genuine uncharged Kerr black hole in the magnetic field, recovering the Wald solution in the weak-field limit. They also analyze the physical properties of the new family, including singularities, horizons, axis regularity, charges, entropy, and temperature.|

|**2026-11-20**|**Twinor Theory in the Newton--Hooke Limit: A Nonrelativistic Schr\"odinger Equation for Arbitrary Spin**|Chen-Hsu Chien|[[arxiv:2611.26379](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26379)]|The paper derives the Newton–Hooke (nonrelativistic) limit of massive twinor field equations at fixed particle mass and fixed cosmological acceleration per unit displacement, aiming to obtain a Schrödinger-type equation valid for arbitrary spin. On a static torsion-free background, removing the rest energy separates the leading spin amplitude from a finite hierarchy of auxiliary spinors; for each rank 2s, the lowest two equations yield a necessary Schrödinger equation with a curvature term and a source from two projections of the relativistic source, while higher equations impose extra constraints. On the homogeneous Newton–Hooke background with trivial internal connection and nonzero cosmological acceleration, vanishing of all limiting contracted sources forces the leading amplitude to vanish for s ≥ 3/2, and a separate reduction of the scalar wave equation covers the spin-zero case. The result clarifies when a consistent nonrelativistic Schrödinger description for arbitrary spin can emerge from twinor theory and highlights strong constraints from the Newton–Hooke background.|
|**2026-11-20**|**Exact Solution for Non-Hermitian Free Fermions: A Case Study of the XY Chain**|Yuguan Li, D. C. Liu, Murray T. Batchelor|[[arxiv:2605.26813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26813)]|The paper addresses the non-Hermitian XY spin chain with open boundary conditions and complex anisotropy, focusing on the exact treatment of exceptional points and defective eigensystems. Using a Chebyshev-polynomial representation with quasi-energy as the spectral variable, the authors prove that all exceptional points are second order for finite even L, that each Chebyshev sector contains at most one double root at fixed coupling, and that the two sectors cannot become defective simultaneously. By retaining the boundary residual, they obtain generalized eigenvectors via differentiation, yielding the Jordan structure and a counting of ordinary and generalized many-body eigenstates; the explicit L=4 case exhibits square-root monodromy, and the normalized left-right overlap is identified with phase rigidity, whose vanishing corresponds to a divergent eigenvalue condition number. These results provide an exact treatment of defective eigensystems and their local spectral sensitivity in a quadratic fermionic model.|

|**2026-11-20**|**Wigner-Dyson correlations in selected sectors of an integrable spin chain**|Bal\'azs Pozsgay|[[arxiv:2611.26263](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26263)]|The paper investigates how spectral statistics can mislead when distinguishing integrable from chaotic quantum many-body systems, using a special integrable spin chain. The authors analyze the model under open and periodic boundary conditions: open boundaries produce homogeneous exponential degeneracies, while periodic boundaries split these degeneracies and yield Wigner-Dyson statistics in a few symmetry-selected sectors—despite the model being clean, translationally invariant, and Yang-Baxter integrable. The work shows that Wigner-Dyson correlations do not necessarily imply chaos, since the model's dynamics remain non-chaotic.|
