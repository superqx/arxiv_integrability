|**2026-09-24**|**Quantum inversion of the vacuum hierarchy and multi-kink unbinding in the non-degenerate double sine-Gordon model**|Jonathan Lozano-Mayo|[[arxiv:2609.24861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24861)]|The paper studies how quantum corrections affect the vacuum energies and multi-kink binding in the non-degenerate double sine-Gordon model, where classically one vacuum is favored over another. Using one-loop calculations with normal ordering at the primary vacuum's meson mass, the authors predict that quantum fluctuations can invert the classical vacuum hierarchy, favoring the secondary central vacuum above a critical coupling. Matrix-product-state simulations for n=4 confirm this inversion and its persistence in the continuum, with the crossing occurring about 22% below the one-loop prediction at α=0.3, closely matching the Gaussian effective potential. Finite-chain results further show that the Q=4 multi-kink's central region expands as vacuum energy differences shrink, indicating incipient unbinding with only a modest correction to its total mass.|
|**2026-09-24**|**Families of Hitchin Systems in Type-D**|Aswin Balasubramanian, Jacques Distler, Ron Donagi, Carlos Perez-Pardavila|[[arxiv:2510.27199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27199)]|The paper addresses the construction of families of Hitchin systems in type-D, extending prior work on type-A cases to describe the Coulomb branch geometry of 4d N=2 SCFTs in class-S. The key difficulty is that type-D constructions involve complicated local constraints at punctures, which the authors analyze to determine their global implications. The main result is a global bundle of spectral (Seiberg-Witten) curves over the conformal manifold of complex structures on the punctured curve. This provides the type-D analogue of the type-A bundle of Hitchin systems, advancing the geometric understanding of conformal manifolds in class-S theories.|
|**2026-09-24**|**Multi-kinks and composite oscillons in a commensurable and non degenerate double sine-Gordon model**|Jonathan Lozano-Mayo, Manuel Torres-Labansat|[[arxiv:2407.09682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09682)]|The paper introduces a commensurable, non-degenerate double sine-Gordon model whose partial breaking of vacuum degeneracy yields stable multi-kinks—configurations of \(n\) localized energy packets with definite separations. It analyzes their collisions and finds that long-lived composite oscillons emerge, reflecting the multi-kink structure, with sub-kink positions and vibration modes used as collective coordinates in a phenomenological model. Linear radiation is shown to synchronize the oscillon's vibrational components, while decay and sudden annihilations arise from a staccato-like mechanism of repeated mass-threshold crossings. This extends soliton phenomenology by linking multi-kink internal structure to composite oscillon dynamics in a controllable model.|

|**2026-09-24**|**The Naito--Okuda--Ouyang Biconcave Red Blood Cell Profile as a Finite-Energy Unbranched Weak Immersion with Point-Force Residues**|Hao Wu|[[arxiv:2609.28278](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28278)]|The Naito–Okuda–Ouyang axisymmetric profile exactly solves the unconstrained Helfrich shape equation but has logarithmically divergent mean curvature at its dimple center, raising questions about its regularity and variational status. Using a covariant local analysis in Cartesian graph coordinates, the authors show the immersion is nondegenerate, lies in \(W^{2,p}\) for all finite \(p\), is \(C^{1,\alpha}\) for \(\alpha<1\), but is neither \(C^{1,1}\) nor \(C^2\), with finite Helfrich energy and branch multiplicity one. The Euler–Lagrange operator carries a Dirac point-force residue \(4\pi c_0\delta_p\), so the profile is a finite-energy weak immersion but not a free critical point of the unforced Canham–Helfrich functional unless a point-force potential or pinned-point constraint is added. These findings clarify the limits of modern existence and regularity theorems for Canham–Helfrich minimizers and reconcile a sign convention discrepancy with the original papers.|
|**2026-09-24**|**Darboux's Theorem in $p$ -adic symplectic geometry**|Luis Crespo, \'Alvaro Pelayo|[[arxiv:2508.15443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15443)]|The paper proves a non-Archimedean analogue of Darboux's theorem, showing that any two symplectic forms on a \(p\)-adic analytic manifold are locally isomorphic. The proof adapts Moser's path method, with the main technical challenge being to show that the resulting flow is given by a power series with nonzero radius of convergence, which requires finding a condition for a long-time solution of an ODE to exist. As a global application, the authors classify second-countable \(p\)-adic analytic symplectic manifolds in terms of \(p\)-adic volume, generalizing a classical theorem of Serre. This establishes that \(p\)-adic phase spaces are locally standard, enabling focus on the dynamics rather than the ambient space.|
