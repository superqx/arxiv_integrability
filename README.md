|**2026-11-17**|**Calculating correlation functions in integrable spin chains from local conserved charges and currents**|Kohei Fukai|[[arxiv:2611.20181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20181)]|The paper addresses the difficulty of computing correlation functions in integrable spin chains by introducing "current minors," antisymmetrized products of local conserved charge densities and their currents, whose expectation values are proven to be determinants of density and current mean values. Local operators are decomposed into these minors, yielding factorized formulas for correlation functions expressed through current mean values that hold in any nondegenerate eigenstate. Applying this to the spin-1/2 XXZ chain and to all representations of the Temperley–Lieb algebra, the authors prove previously conjectured factorized formulas for short-distance correlation functions. The work provides a simple algebraic framework that unifies and rigorously establishes earlier conjectures.|
|**2026-11-17**|**Renormalization Group Flow from 4d Chern-Simons Theory**|Meer Ashwinkumar, Masahito Yamazaki|[[arxiv:2611.19517](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19517)]|The paper addresses how two-dimensional integrable quantum field theories realized by four-dimensional Chern-Simons theory on Σ × C behave under renormalization group flow, aiming to derive this flow from first principles within the 4d framework. Using Feynman diagram computations, the authors derive the one-loop RG flow for theories with order surface defects—where affine Kac-Moody symmetry underlies the flow—and, within the Batalin-Vilkovisky formalism, for the principal chiral model without Wess-Zumino term realized via disorder defects. They recover the known asymptotically-free beta function of the PCM through two complementary methods: explicit evaluation of one-loop amplitudes and an analogue of analytic torsion for 4d Chern-Simons theory. This establishes a direct 4d derivation of 2d RG flows, connecting defect symmetries and topological invariants to integrable QFT dynamics.|
|**2026-11-17**|**Quantum vacuum inversion and multi-kink unbinding in the non-degenerate double sine-Gordon model**|Jonathan Lozano-Mayo|[[arxiv:2609.24861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24861)]|The paper addresses how quantum fluctuations can unbind composite solitons (multi-kinks) in the non-degenerate double sine-Gordon model, even when mass corrections remain small. Using one-loop analysis, tensor-network lattice calculations, and the Gaussian effective potential, the authors show that the vacuum at the multi-kink's center becomes the true vacuum at a coupling of order the classical vacuum lifting, and that this inversion persists beyond one loop with a well-defined continuum limit. On finite chains, the multi-kink unbinds when the tension of the central vacuum string between its two halves drops through zero. The results establish a non-perturbative mechanism for soliton unbinding and identify the Gaussian effective potential as a reliable tool for capturing it.|
