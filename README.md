|**2026-11-16**|**Multiparameter Quantum Affine Spaces and the Scalene Yang--Baxter Equation**|Pramod Padmanabhan, Somnath Maity, Vladimir Korepin|[[arxiv:2608.20714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20714)]|The paper addresses the construction of solutions to the non-braided scalene Yang–Baxter equation, a setting where standard constant Yang–Baxter techniques do not directly apply. Starting from two anticommuting generators, the authors build representation-independent solution families from quadratic noncommutative algebras, deform them via quantum-plane algebras, and extend the construction to arbitrarily many generators, where the scalene relation forces the multiparameter exchange matrix into an exact multiplicative form and singles out a distinguished class of multiparameter quantum affine spaces. They further provide finite-dimensional realizations using singular matrices and finite Heisenberg–Weyl operators, and infinite-dimensional ones via bilateral weighted and multiplicative-shift operators, showing that these are generically weakly purely scalene and, for selected finite-dimensional cases, strongly pure by obstructing a common meromorphic Yang–Baxter parametrization. The significance lies in supplying an algebraic, representation-independent framework for building scalene triples and a foundation for exploring their use in quantum integrability.|
|**2026-11-16**|**Collective superintegrable systems from the Guillemin--Sternberg torus action**|L. Feher|[[arxiv:2608.01878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01878)]|The paper addresses the superintegrability of collective Hamiltonians invariant under a compact connected semisimple Lie group action on a symplectic manifold. The authors construct the Abelian Poisson algebra generated by invariant functions of the momentum map and analyze its centralizer, using a Guillemin–Sternberg Hamiltonian torus action to establish a dimension identity: the functional dimensions of these algebras sum to the dimension of the manifold. Combined with a non-triviality condition, this identity proves that the algebra forms a superintegrable system and that the momentum map of the GS torus action provides action variables. These results complement earlier work by Bolsinov and Jovanović and offer new insight into collective superintegrability.|

|**2026-11-16**|**Correlation functions of the Lieb--Liniger Bose gas in equilibrium states: a microscopic approach to space-time asymptotics**|Frank G\"ohmann, Karol K. Kozlowski, Mikhail D. Minin|[[arxiv:2611.18316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18316)]|The paper addresses the long-time, large-distance asymptotics of correlation functions in quantum integrable models, a problem typically tackled only through heuristic methods like generalized hydrodynamics. The authors develop an approximation-free approach to analyze form-factor series in the thermodynamic limit for equilibrium states described by stationary density matrices, exemplifying it with the Lieb–Liniger Bose gas. For two-point density and current-density correlators, their results confirm the phenomenological GHD predictions, while for a field-conjugated field correlation function they uncover behavior governed by a novel space- and time-dependent nonlinear integral equation. This provides a rigorous microscopic foundation for space-time asymptotics and extends reach beyond what heuristic methods can access.|
|**2026-11-16**|**Group classification of a class of generalized Korteweg-de Vries equations**|Oleksandra O. Vinnichenko|[[arxiv:2611.18247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18247)]|The paper addresses the group classification problem for a class of generalized Korteweg–de Vries equations with arbitrary convection and dispersion coefficients depending on the dependent variable, a class encompassing KdV, modified KdV, Harry Dym, and Rosenau–Hyman compacton equations. Using the method of furcate splitting, the authors perform a complete group classification of the class, compute its equivalence group in two independent ways (via an advanced direct method and a megaideal-based algebraic method), and construct the automorphism group of the equivalence algebra along with its infinitely many megaideals. They find that the maximal Lie invariance algebras of equations in the class have dimensions from two to five, with all symmetry extensions regular except those for Harry Dym-type equations, which are singular and uniquely of maximal dimension five, and they also classify conservation laws with characteristics of nonpositive order and identify all additional point equivalences between classification cases. The results provide a systematic and rigorous symmetry framework for this widely used family of nonlinear dispersive wave models.|
|**2026-11-16**|**On transition probabilities for the ASEP on the half-line**|Alexandr Garbali, Cengiz Gazi, Jan de Gier, William Mead, Michael Wheeler|[[arxiv:2611.12258](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12258)]|The paper studies the asymmetric simple exclusion process on the half-line with particle insertion and no removal at the boundary, focusing on transition probabilities between finite configurations with the same particle count. It identifies two distinct multiple contour integral formulas for this quantity, recalling one from the literature and proving a new second formula via a coordinate Bethe ansatz verification of the evolution equation and initial condition. As an application, summing over all final configurations yields an integral formula for the probability that particle number is conserved up to a given time. The proofs rely on symmetric rational functions and contour deformations.|
|**2026-11-16**|**Fer expansion of driven spins: closed-form recursion and no-go theorems for termination**|Lorenzo Bagnasacco, Daniel Burgarth, Vittorio Giovannetti, Kazuya Yuasa|[[arxiv:2610.27979](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27979)]|The Fer expansion factorizes a driven quantum propagator into unitary exponential factors, but each effective Hamiltonian involves an infinite nested-commutator series, making truncation and termination unclear. For su(2) in any representation, the authors represent these Hamiltonians as time-dependent vectors in R^3 and resum the series exactly into a closed recursion involving single and double cross products with the time integral, weighted by elementary functions. Using this map, they show the expansion can stop finitely only when the leftover evolution is known in closed form, e.g., when the effective Hamiltonian commutes with itself or precesses about a fixed axis at constant angular velocity. For real-analytic drives, they prove neither occurs beyond the first factor unless the first exponential is already exact, so terminating the expansion yields no new exactly solvable drives.|
|**2026-11-16**|**Multiparameter Quantum Affine Spaces and the Scalene Yang--Baxter Equation**|Pramod Padmanabhan, Somnath Maity, Vladimir Korepin|[[arxiv:2608.20714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20714)]|The paper addresses the construction of representation-independent solutions to the non-braided scalene Yang–Baxter equation, a setting where standard constant Yang–Baxter techniques do not directly apply. |**2026-11-16**|**Collective superintegrable systems from the Guillemin--Sternberg torus action**|L. Feher|[[arxiv:2608.01878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01878)]|The paper addresses the superintegrability of collective Hamiltonians invariant under a Hamiltonian action of a compact connected semisimple Lie group on a symplectic manifold, building on and complementing earlier work by Bolsinov and Jovanović. The authors study the Abelian Poisson algebra generated by invariant functions of the momentum map together with its Poisson centralizer, and they exploit a Hamiltonian torus action due to Guillemin and Sternberg to analyze these algebras. Their main result shows that the functional dimensions of the algebra and its centralizer sum to the dimension of the manifold, which, under a non-triviality condition, establishes superintegrability and implies that the momentum map of the Guillemin–Sternberg torus action provides action variables. This gives a new geometric route to collective superintegrability and a constructive way to obtain action-angle variables.|
