Villanueva|[[arxiv:2608.06639](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06639)]|This paper introduces a new model for a static magnetic black hole using nonlinear electrodynamics (NLED). Instead of assuming a regular center, the model enforces physically motivated conditions like a Maxwell weak-field limit, finite self-energy, and causal light cones, which constrain the theory's exponent. |**2026-08-11**|**Entanglement and non-local magic in a non-unitarily deformed non-Hermitian bipartite system**|Chen-Huan Wu|[[arxiv:2608.08346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08346)]|This paper introduces a bipartite model that separates non-Hermitian spectral degeneracies from eigenvector properties like entanglement. By applying an invertible non-unitary similarity transformation to a Hermitian operator, the authors create a non-Hermitian Hamiltonian with a non-defective degeneracy, allowing right eigenstates to evolve smoothly from product to maximally entangled states without spectral changes. Using an exactly solvable two-qubit example, they show entanglement grows monotonically with the non-Hermiticity parameter, while non-local magic peaks at intermediate coupling, vanishing at both extremes. The work provides clean diagnostics for distinguishing degeneracy types, entanglement typicality, and non-stabilizer correlations, offering a basis-independent framework for studying non-Hermitian quantum systems.|

The authors prove that despite this loss of independence, the increments exhibit a one-sided negative association across arbitrary directions. As a key application, they derive an exponential concentration inequality for sums of these increments on the diffusive scale, even without independence. While the argument relies on a specific Burke property for exponential weights, the other proof techniques—such as LPP monotonicities and braid relations—are applicable to general weight distributions, highlighting the broader relevance of the method.|

They find that for a decreasing coupling in the adiabatic regime, a time-dependent mass gap emerges, exponentially decaying with time and matching the functional form of the static theory's non-perturbative mass scale, thus establishing a time-dependent analogue of dynamical dimensional transmutation. For very fast drives or large times, the system becomes asymptotically free, approaching the $SU(2)_1$ WZNW model at the UV fixed point, demonstrating a dynamic realization of the static theory's fixed-point structure.|

The authors apply their construction to the Moore-Read and Read-Rezayi states, showing that the corresponding Jack-polynomial wave functions are exact zero modes of these new continuum Hamiltonians. |**2026-08-11**|**Quantum Integrability of Hamiltonians with Time-Dependent Interaction Strengths and the Renormalization Group Flow**|Parameshwar R. Solving the time-dependent Schrödinger equation yields quantum Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov (qKZ) equations, whose consistency fixes the temporal trajectories of the coupling constants. The key result is that the existence of just one such conservation law rules out "partially integrable" systems with a finite but large number of conserved charges. This finding provides a rigorous foundation for identifying new integrable models and deepens the theoretical understanding of conservation laws in quantum many-body systems.|

For the two-component model, they further demonstrate that this unique ground state has zero spin projection and, when hopping coefficients are real, forms a spin singlet. The results are significant because they apply to any finite lattice and generalize Lieb’s theorem to these bosonic systems, providing rigorous insight into their quantum phases.|
