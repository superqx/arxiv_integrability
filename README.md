|Publish Date|Title|Authors|arXiv|Summary|

|**2026-10-08**|**Black hole radial geometry from a quantum spin chain**|Boris A. Khanikati|[[arxiv:2610.10504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10504)]|The paper addresses how to encode the radial geometry of an anti-de Sitter black brane in a quantum spin chain. It shows that the radial equation for a scalar field maps onto an exactly solvable semi-infinite spin chain, with the radial coordinate emerging as lattice quasimomentum and spin coherence encoding the geometry. The authors implement the finite chain on a superconducting processor and find that at low energies it yields a conformal field theory in a coordinate proportional to proper radial distance, while also recovering gravitational observables and exact screening-mass sum rules. This establishes a concrete, experimentally realizable quantum-simulation link between spin-chain dynamics and black hole radial geometry.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Chiral Extensions of Cotangent Yangian**|Keyou Zeng|[[arxiv:2610.10202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10202)]|The paper addresses the lack of a vertex-algebraic realization of the cotangent Yangian by constructing chiral extensions using vertex algebroids. At zero level this yields a vertex algebra with a compatible coproduct whose Zhu reduction recovers the Abedin–Niu cotangent Yangian, and extensions at nonzero levels with level-additive coproducts are also built. The zero-level algebra carries two quasi-conformal structures, for which the sphere one-point conformal blocks are computed and given associative products: the first block space is identified with the dual of a Lie algebroid homology, while the second is described via mini-twistor geometry.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Algebraic Proofs of Quantum Period--TBA Relations**|Min-xin Huang|[[arxiv:2610.09922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09922)]|The paper proves two conjectural relations connecting quantum A-periods of mirror curves to TBA-like equations, which previously lacked rigorous justification. The quadratic case is derived from constant terms in shifted product expansions, while higher-degree cases use a cyclic quantum-torus trace and a finite matrix determinant to express the period as the free energy of nonoverlapping rods, whose density equation produces the TBA-like equation with normalization set by rod length. Both proofs hold as formal power series in the complex structure parameter with exact dependence on \(q=e^{i\hbar}\). This establishes the quantum period–TBA correspondence on firm algebraic footing.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Semiclassical Cosmological Constant in Anti-de Sitter and de Sitter Spacetimes**|Milton C. Mamani-Leqque|[[arxiv:2610.09436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09436)]|The paper studies how quantum backreaction from a scalar field modifies the cosmological constant in four-dimensional anti-de Sitter (AdS) and de Sitter (dS) spacetimes. The authors compute self-consistent field parameters and the renormalized vacuum stress-energy tensor to derive an effective cosmological constant, finding that for massless fields it takes the universal form Λ_eff = A/ℓ² + B/ℓ⁴ in both cases. In AdS the solutions depend on boundary conditions, while the dS results (in the Bunch-Davies state) are sensitive to global and state-dependent information. Testing the classical AdS-dS continuation ℓ → iℓ at the quantum level, they find the quantum contributions fail to map correctly—coefficients differ (29/61 for minimal coupling) or have opposite signs (conformal coupling)—showing that semiclassical backreaction is not fixed by local curvature alone and limiting analytic continuation as a bridge between the two quantum theories.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Root systems and Minimal RG flows to c=1 CFTs**|Changrim Ahn, Sara Kostadinovic, Francesco Ravanini|[[arxiv:2610.08938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08938)]|The paper constructs exact massless S-matrices from algebraic root-system geometry and derives thermodynamic Bethe ansatz equations that generate minimal renormalization group flows. It extends P. Dorey's geometric construction, originally for massive S-matrices, to the massless case and proves these new S-matrices satisfy the bootstrap equations. Focusing on minimal \(D_n\) coset conformal field theories—identified as \(c=1\) free-boson CFTs with compactification radius \(\sqrt{n/2}\)—the authors determine their ultraviolet completions by analyzing the TBA. This work thus links root-system geometry to the classification and UV structure of massless integrable RG flows.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Disorder at the Cosmological Collider: Quenched Random Fields**|Matheus Curado Ferreira|[[arxiv:2609.30095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30095)]|Massive particles produced during inflation generate oscillatory "cosmological collider" signals in primordial correlators, but these are typically modeled assuming a clean, deterministic source. This paper studies a heavy spectator field driven by a spatially quenched random source, representing a frozen light field or environmental sector with relaxation time longer than the inflationary timescale, and solves the fixed-realization dynamics exactly for a power-law source using Lommel functions. After Gaussian disorder averaging, the random source affects only the statistical part of the propagator while leaving the spectral function and heavy-field poles intact, so the oscillation frequency (and hence the inferred mass) remains robust, but the amplitude, phase, and envelope of the signal depend on the source's temporal profile. The significance is that mass measurements from collider signals survive this class of quenched disorder, whereas amplitude-based inferences can be strongly modified—including enhancement of the nonanalytic clock signal by decaying sources or destructive interference from spatial white noise.|

Crowdy|[[arxiv:2610.10146](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10146)]|The paper extends a known mathematical analogy between two distinct classes of 2D free-boundary problems in inviscid fluid mechanics: flows with constant vorticity and no surface tension, and irrotational flows with capillarity. It demonstrates that a hollow vortex equilibrium in a shear flow with third-order irrotational strain corresponds mathematically to a steady bubble with surface tension in a simple straining flow. For both problems the authors construct an exact isolated solution at a parameter value γ = 0 and obtain numerical solutions for nonzero γ, revealing conformal mapping identities from the capillary class that also apply to the hollow vortex case under appropriate parameter constraints. This widens the scope of the analogy and provides new tractable connections between vortical and capillary free-boundary problems.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Polynomial Difference Schemes with Energy and Momentum Multipliers on Orthogonal Uniform Meshes: Stencil Restrictions and Monge--Amp\`ere-Type Examples**|E. I. Kaptsov|[[arxiv:2610.08979](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08979)]|The paper investigates whether nonlinear polynomial finite-difference schemes on orthogonal uniform meshes can simultaneously conserve discrete analogues of energy and momentum when the multipliers are the standard difference approximations of \(u_t\) and \(u_x\). For the 9-point stencil, the authors prove this is impossible for polynomials of arbitrary degree, while on larger stencils they show via a difference analogue of the direct method that such schemes exist only for bounded polynomial degrees and approximate second-order Monge–Ampère-type equations. As a by-product, they construct a scheme for the homogeneous Monge–Ampère equation that has five conservation laws, retains most symmetries of the differential equation, and is invariant under the discrete dihedral group \(D_4\), with numerical tests confirming second-order accuracy and conservation-law satisfaction on exact solutions.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Proof of the absence of local conserved quantities in the Holstein model**|Fuga Ishii, Mizuki Sanatani|[[arxiv:2605.19606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19606)]|The paper addresses the lack of a rigorous proof of nonintegrability in electron-phonon coupled systems, a common setting in condensed matter physics where such absence is usually assumed rather than demonstrated. The authors prove that the one-dimensional Holstein model possesses no nontrivial local conserved quantities besides the Hamiltonian and total fermion number, extending this result to the Holstein-Hubbard model and to higher-dimensional hypercubic lattices. This work is significant because it broadens nonintegrability proofs to systems mixing particles with different statistical properties, providing a firmer foundation for assumptions about thermalization and transport in electron-phonon models.|

Although measurement outcomes leak partial information about local conserved charges in a finite subsystem, the authors argue this information becomes negligible in the thermodynamic limit, leading to a fermionic Gaussian Scrooge ensemble fixed by the subsystem's generalized Gibbs ensemble. They construct this ensemble directly at the subsystem level and numerically confirm it captures the late-time projected ensemble at finite temperature. This extends thermalization descriptions for non-interacting integrable systems from reduced density matrices to universal statistics of conditional pure states.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Inhomogeneous cluster DMFT study on p-n junction Mott gap closing in Hubbard lattice**|Florian Brette, Eunjae Jeong, Hongchul Choi, Ji Hoon Shim, Geunsik Lee|[[arxiv:2610.09453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09453)]|This paper studies how an external potential gradient closes the Mott gap in a Hubbard square lattice, focusing on whether the drop direction (along the square edge versus the diagonal) affects the insulator-to-metal transition. The authors model a p-n junction of hole- and electron-doped domains with smoothly varying on-site potential, solving the inhomogeneous problem using cluster dynamical mean-field theory with 2×2 plaquette impurities. They find that the metallic phase emerges when the potential drop becomes comparable to the 2×2 plaquette excitation energy (~0.35t at U = 5.8t), with a threshold nearly independent of direction, but the resulting correlated metal shows a stronger zero-energy coherent peak for the diagonal drop due to preserved degeneracy of two molecular orbitals. The authors introduce a wavefunction readout that maps continuum neural quantum states onto site-resolved charge and spin statistics, allowing effective degrees of freedom to emerge from the microscopic solution. Applied to moiré transition metal dichalcogenides at filling ν = 3, this reveals a low-spin ground state below the ferromagnetic crystal of spin-polarized Wigner molecules, with charge fluctuations via bound nearest-neighbor doublon–hole pairs and spin carried by rim electrons around inert spin-singlet cores. These intertwined charge and spin fluctuations, with antiferromagnetic inter-site correlations, motivate an effective spinful two-orbital Hubbard model, demonstrating a general route from continuum wavefunctions to effective lattice models when competing scales preclude controlled reduction.|
|**2026-10-08**|**Exact solutions for kinematics across interaction-deformed Fermi surfaces**|Alexander Kruchkov, F. Duncan M. The authors derive exact identities for how the occupation deficit inside and surplus outside the interacting Fermi surface change when an interaction is switched on at fixed density, expressing each as a surface-swept weight plus a change in Luttinger's integrand at fixed momentum. These results clarify how quasiparticle weight is transferred across a deforming Fermi surface and hold independently of whether Luttinger's theorem is satisfied.|
