|**2026-10-16**|**Gram--Wishart--Stiefel formulation of the $N=2$, large--$d$ gauge theory in 1D: towards emergent Grassmannian geometry**|Badis Ydri|[[arxiv:2607.08481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08481)]|The paper reformulates the $N=2$, large-$d$ endpoint theory of lattice BFSS/BMN matrix quantum mechanics using Gram/Wishart/Stiefel variables, expressing holonomy invariants in radial and angular data and showing that the aligned contribution shifts the effective mass to $\alpha_\Lambda - 1/2$. By solving the pure $-A$ theory exactly, the authors identify a rank-two Bessel kernel that captures the $O(2)$ angular sector and correct the spurious doubled Wishart entropy. They show that apparent perturbativity bounds from finite polynomial truncations are artifacts, and that summing the transverse completion recovers the Wishart saddle with the physical shifted mass, reproducing the universal $-2d$ contribution of the $D_\Lambda$-channel. The work outlines an emergent Grassmannian geometry from the two endpoint planes, whose relative angular fluctuations form a $2(d-2)$-dimensional sector, and notes that genuinely anisotropic $\beta_\Lambda$-channel physics lies beyond a pure transverse $B$ -type description.|

|**2026-10-16**|**Singular wave functions and quantum collapse in inverse-power potentials**|Hidetsugu Sakaguchi, Boris Malomed|[[arxiv:2610.21044](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21044)]|The paper addresses quantum collapse in inverse-power potentials \(U(r) = -U_0/(2r^n)\) within the Schrödinger equation, where the ground state is absent and stationary wave functions oscillate infinitely near the origin. The authors derive analytical asymptotic forms of singular stationary wave functions at \(r \to 0\), which form a continuous spectrum, along with some exact solutions under specific conditions. They also study a regularized potential truncated at a finite distance from the center, finding that the wave functions then exhibit a finite number of oscillations, the missing ground state reappears, and the bound-state spectrum becomes discrete. These results clarify the nature of singular quantum states and show how regularization restores physically sensible bound-state structure.|
|**2026-10-16**|**Sparse anisotropic positive maps for qutrit entanglement: exact indecomposability and PPT geometry**|Massimiliano F. Sacchi|[[arxiv:2607.12470](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12470)]|The paper addresses the challenge of detecting entanglement beyond the PPT criterion by introducing a two-parameter family of sparse bistochastic positive maps on qutrits with independently tunable coherence channels. Using this exactly solvable structure, the authors derive analytic boundaries for positivity, complete positivity, and decomposability, showing that indecomposability arises outside a quarter circle and certifying it with explicit PPT entangled states. At a special endpoint they construct rank-(5,5) PPT edge states whose rays form exposed faces of the PPT cone, and they provide an optimal refinement with a strictly larger detection region. This provides a rare analytically tractable qutrit framework unifying positivity, indecomposability, PPT entanglement, optimality, and convex geometry.|
|**2026-10-16**|**Gram--Wishart--Stiefel formulation of the $N=2$, large--$d$ gauge theory in 1D: towards emergent Grassmannian geometry**|Badis Ydri|[[arxiv:2607.08481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08481)]|The paper reformulates the large-\(d\), \(N=2\) planar endpoint theory of lattice BFSS/BMN matrix quantum mechanics using Gram–Wishart–Stiefel variables, expressing holonomy invariants through rank-two Wishart eigenvalues and relative Stiefel angles. It shows that the aligned \(-A\) contribution can be absorbed into the Gaussian sector with a shifted effective mass \(\alpha_{\rm eff}=\alpha_\Lambda-1/2\), with the angular integral encoded in a rank-two Bessel kernel. Finite polynomial truncations give an apparent perturbativity bound that is shown to be a truncation artifact; after summing the transverse completion and balancing the \(+A\) term, the Wishart saddle and universal \(-2d\) continuum contribution are recovered. The work outlines an emergent Grassmannian geometry from the two endpoint planes, whose relative angular fluctuations define a \(2(d-2)\)-dimensional sector.|
|**2026-10-16**|**Construction of Gross-Neveu model using Polchinski flow equation**|Pawe{\l} Duch|[[arxiv:2403.18562](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18562)]|The paper addresses the construction of the Gross-Neveu model, a fermionic asymptotically free and scaling-critical quantum field theory, which had previously only been handled via perturbative or expansion-based methods. The authors develop a new approach to the Polchinski renormalization group flow equation, formulating it as a well-posed infinite-dimensional PDE on a Banach space and solving it directly through a fixed-point argument that relies on a scale decomposition of the free fermionic field in a filtered noncommutative probability space. As a result, they rigorously prove convergence of the Schwinger functions as the cutoff is removed and construct a random field whose moments match these Schwinger functions, giving the first explicit strong coupling between free and interacting fields for a scaling-critical theory. The method is expected to extend to other critical or subcritical models, including symplectic fermions and sine-Gordon.|

|**2026-10-16**|**Parafermions in polarized $\nu=2/3$ Fractional Quantum (Anomalous) Hall Heterostructures**|Steffen Bollmann, Andreas Haller, Thomas L. Schmidt, Jukka I. V\"ayrynen, Elio J. Using bosonization and renormalization group analysis, the authors identify the parameter regimes where interedge dipole interactions gap the neutral sector, leaving a charge sector governed by a double sine-Gordon model that hosts parafermions at domain walls. They find that clean systems require fine-tuned interaction strength and range, whereas correlated disorder—expected for smooth disorder potentials—substantially enlarges the parameter regime supporting these modes, while short-ranged uncorrelated disorder suppresses them. This provides a more experimentally accessible route to Z₃ parafermions in polarized ν=2/3 heterostructures.|
|**2026-10-16**|**Extended strange metal and superconductivity in partially filled moir\'e Hubbard bands**|Yiyu Xia, Yichi Zhang, Zhongdong Han, Hongyuan Li, Brandon Bauer, Kenji Watanabe, Takashi Taniguchi, Wenjin Zhao, Jie Shan, Kin Fai Mak|[[arxiv:2610.22307](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22307)]|In twisted bilayer WSe₂, the authors investigate the largely unexplored regime of a partially filled moiré Hubbard band away from half-filling, where intermediate correlation strength may host novel phases. Using dual-gated transport devices with tunable twist angle and electric field, they identify an extended strange metal that persists over a wide doping and twist-angle range, survives down to 100 mK, and spans multiple symmetry-broken ground states. They also discover a new superconducting dome (SC2) emerging from this strange metal near ν ≈ 0.55, far from both the Mott insulator and the van Hove singularity. These results establish the partially filled Hubbard band in the clean limit as a distinct platform for strange-metal physics and unconventional superconductivity.|
|**2026-10-16**|**$d$-wave superconductivity in quintuple-layer bulk superconductor La$_6$Ni$_5$O$_{12}$**|Zhan Wang, Yuxin Wang, Hui-Ke Jin, Kun Jiang, Jiangping Hu|[[arxiv:2610.22212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22212)]|The recent discovery of bulk superconductivity in the quintuple-layer nickelate La₆Ni₅O₁₂ raises the question of its pairing symmetry. Using an effective five-layer Hubbard model and variational Monte Carlo calculations, the authors find that a spin-singlet d_{x²−y²}-wave state is energetically favored over extended-s-wave pairing. The optimized state reproduces multiple experimental signatures of a clean nodal superconductor, including a V-shaped tunneling density of states, linear low-temperature superfluid depletion, a decreasing Knight shift, and 1/T₁T ∝ T². Extending the analysis to other reduced Ruddlesden–Popper nickelates reveals a robust d-wave tendency in infinite-layer LaNiO₂ and a d-wave/non-superconducting boundary in trilayer La₄Ni₃O₈, suggesting a common d-wave pairing tendency across this family.|
|**2026-10-16**|**Localization induced by Sinai disorder in mass-exchange models**|Michele Giusfredi, Luca Avena, Paolo Politi|[[arxiv:2610.21777](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21777)]|The paper introduces a conservative continuous-mass-exchange model on a finite segment with quenched disorder in the bond splitting laws, addressing how such disorder affects the stationary mass distribution. Despite generically irreversible dynamics, the stationary mean mass profile is exactly solvable via an associated birth-and-death chain and admits a Gibbs representation using a simple random-walk potential. The authors show that the parameter β√N separates three regimes—asymptotically flat, Brownian crossover, and localized near the global minimum—and derive arcsine laws for the localization centre, left-right valley competition, and disorder-averaged mass measure. Near the localization transition at β=0, the inverse participation ratio for extremal masses exhibits a k^{-3/2} decay and a scaling form, linking the results to conditioned random walks and establishing disorder-induced localization in mass-exchange dynamics.|
|**2026-10-16**|**Quantum oscillations in a solvable model of a Kondo insulator**|Abhisek Samanta, Mohit Randeria, Sumilan Banerjee|[[arxiv:2610.20734](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20734)]|The paper investigates how strong electron interactions affect quantum oscillations in a Kondo insulator, a problem relevant to interpreting anomalous oscillation experiments in strongly correlated insulators. The authors introduce a generalized periodic Anderson model with SYK interactions at each site, solvable in the large-N limit, and derive interaction-corrected Lifshitz-Kosevich formulae. They find that the insulating state remains stable even for interactions exceeding the non-interacting gap, that dHvA frequencies are unrenormalized while SdH frequencies shift only weakly, and that dHvA amplitudes are essentially unaffected whereas SdH amplitudes are strongly enhanced by gap renormalization. These results provide a tractable benchmark clarifying how interactions may reshape quantum oscillation signatures in Kondo insulators.|

These results establish compressibility as a practical control parameter for managing density and turbulent flow in active nematic systems.|
