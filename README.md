Key results from the Yang-Lee spin chain show that statically calibrated boundary data accurately predict the timing of antilinear-paired correlators to a high relative precision, and that preparation phases control local evolution and analytic continuation. This establishes a universal BCFT framework for biorthogonal quenches, organizing post-quench dynamics via complex temporal-boundary data.|

Pasnoori|[[arxiv:2608.19519](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19519)]|This paper extends the study of integrability-preserving time-dependent driving in quantum systems from Hermitian to non-Hermitian Hamiltonians with time-varying interaction strengths. Using a generalized Bethe ansatz framework, the authors show that the constraints for maintaining integrability are broader than the standard renormalization group (RG) protocol, which equates time with the logarithm of the cutoff. Specifically, RG-invariant interaction strengths in the static model can either remain constant or adopt a specific time-dependence in the driven model, as dictated by integrability conditions. They identify 16 minimal three-site models from this chain, finding that six are integrable and two are generically nonintegrable, with the remaining four forming a compact classification after imposing one-site translational symmetry. The results reveal a local mechanism where coupling integrable structures destroys integrability beyond nearest-neighbor interactions.|

These exact solutions provide a valuable benchmark for testing numerical methods and for studying quantum tunneling phenomena in systems with finite-range potentials.|

|**2026-08-21**|**Nonlinear Drude weight of the one-dimensional Hubbard model**|Tetsuya Iwasaki, Hosho Katsura|[[arxiv:2608.20269](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20269)]|This paper studies nonlinear Drude weights (NLDWs) in the one-dimensional repulsive Hubbard model, focusing on their behavior at quarter and half filling. The authors combine exact Bethe-ansatz calculations with low-energy effective field theory to derive and verify NLDW expressions, finding that band-curvature corrections contribute finitely while Umklapp interactions predict divergences that are contradicted by exact results. At half filling, they analyze finite-size scaling across the Mott transition, deriving asymptotic behavior in the insulating phase and proposing a hyperscaling ansatz near the critical point. They identify the necessary dipolar orientations and ladder geometries to realize this phase, which could be detected experimentally through time-of-flight measurements. They demonstrate that explicit symmetry breaking cannot be determined from the order-parameter Hamiltonian alone, requiring additional couplings. |**2026-08-21**|**Dissipation-driven champion solitons in one-dimensional shallow-water waves**|Ashleigh Simonis, Sergey Nazarenko, Jalal Shatah, Yulin Pan|[[arxiv:2608.20006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20006)]|This paper investigates rogue wave formation in shallow-water waves using the Kaup-Boussinesq equation. The authors introduce a weak, high-wavenumber dissipative perturbation, which breaks the system's integrability and causes dominant solitons to grow by absorbing energy from weaker, co-propagating solitons. They find that this "champion soliton" intensification requires both dissipation and the presence of random waves, which catalyze energy transfer. The authors employ a two-qubit composite representation to prove this chain is nonintegrable for any nonzero deformation, even though it is built from integrable components. By extracting minimal injective models, they classify sixteen cases, finding six that are integrable and two that are nonintegrable under specific criteria, which reduces to four genuine three-site models in standard spin chains. The significance lies in providing a compact classification that reveals a local mechanism where coupling integrable building blocks can destroy integrability beyond nearest-neighbor interactions.|
