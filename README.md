|**2026-09-25**|**Quenched Cosmological Collider Physics: Random fields & white noises**|Matheus Curado Ferreira|[[arxiv:2609.30095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30095)]|The paper studies how a massive spectator field in de Sitter space responds to a linearly coupled, spatially quenched random source with a power-law time profile, motivated by cosmological collider signatures. It solves the fixed-realization dynamics exactly using Lommel functions and a Mellin–Barnes representation, then performs Gaussian disorder averaging, showing that the randomness affects only the statistical part of the propagator while leaving the spectral function and heavy-field poles intact. The resulting collider signal factorizes into one generalized hypergeometric forced-response sector and two Gauss hypergeometric massive branches, with the nonanalytic folded contribution cancelling; persistent sources generate a late-time logarithm in the bispectrum, while decaying sources make the Schwinger–Keldysh endpoint integrable and can make the massive clock dominate in the squeezed limit. These results show that the source's temporal profile controls the clock's envelope, amplitude, and phase but not its logarithmic frequency, which remains fixed by the heavy-field mass, with implications for disorder effects in cosmological correlators.|
|**2026-09-25**|**Quantum vacuum inversion and multi-kink unbinding in the non-degenerate double sine-Gordon model**|Jonathan Lozano-Mayo|[[arxiv:2609.24861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24861)]|In the non-degenerate double sine-Gordon model, a composite soliton can unbind when quantum corrections invert the ordering of the vacua inside and outside it. Working at one loop with normal ordering at the primary-vacuum meson mass, the authors show that the central secondary vacuum becomes the true vacuum at a coupling of order the classical vacuum splitting, with the tension of a secondary-vacuum string carrying the logarithm of the meson-mass ratio. Matrix-product-state calculations for the $Q=4$ multi-kink confirm this inversion, match the Gaussian effective potential, and reveal the internal string lengthening and filling the box past the crossing, while the soliton mass correction stays below a tenth of its classical value.|
|**2026-09-25**|**Tsunami Solitons Emerging from Superconducting Gap**|Daisuke A. Takahashi|[[arxiv:2508.18311](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18311)]|The paper proposes a classical integrable system that produces tsunami-like solitons on a disordered background, with one Lax operator interpretable as a Bogoliubov–de Gennes Hamiltonian for parity-mixed superconductors. Using Krichever's method, the authors derive a family of integrable equations whose pure s-wave limit recovers the coupled Schrödinger–Boussinesq hierarchy from plasma physics. They show that a linearly unstable finite background with a superconducting gap supports tsunami solitons—where a step structure reverses propagation while oscillating on the opposite side—as well as inhomogeneous stationary solutions called "KdV rocks." These results connect soliton dynamics to Bogoliubov quasiparticles in the energy gap, with the large space of stationary solutions arising from the non-coprime Lax pair and multivalued Baker–Akhiezer functions, and they introduce "isodispersive phases" to classify quasiperiodic multi-tsunami backgrounds.|

|**2026-09-25**|**A Human-AI Theorem Connecting Spontaneous and Field-Induced Mechanisms of Collective Behavior in One Dimension**|Weiguo Yin|[[arxiv:2609.00322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00322)]|The paper investigates whether AI can generate scientific hypotheses beyond a human collaborator's active hypothesis space, documenting a human-AI collaboration that proved a theorem linking zero-field collective behavior from competing interactions to field-induced behavior in one dimension. The theorem establishes an exact Hamiltonian-level mapping between a zero-field O(n)-vector chain with nearest- and next-nearest-neighbor interactions and a simpler chain with nearest-neighbor coupling plus a single-spin potential, generalizing to q-state Potts spins. The proof, enabled by an AI-synthesized recursive Householder moving frame, resolved a longstanding challenge for n=3 posed in 1990 and yielded a closed-form solution for the J1-J2 Potts chain. |**2026-09-25**|**Entanglement and non-local magic in a non-unitarily deformed non-Hermitian bipartite system**|Chen-Huan Wu|[[arxiv:2608.08346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08346)]|The paper addresses how entanglement and non-Hermitian degeneracies are distinct phenomena, since entanglement depends on eigenvectors rather than eigenvalues alone. The authors construct a bipartite model in which an invertible non-unitary similarity transformation deforms a Hermitian operator with a degenerate eigenspace, yielding a non-Hermitian Hamiltonian that remains non-defective at all finite non-Hermiticity strengths. In an exactly solvable two-qubit case, the right eigenstates continuously transition from product states to maximally entangled states while the spectrum stays fixed, and entanglement increases monotonically with the non-Hermiticity parameter, whereas non-local magic peaks at intermediate coupling and vanishes in both the product and maximally entangled limits. This framework cleanly separates non-defective degeneracy, exceptional-point sensitivity, Haar-typical entanglement, and non-stabilizer correlations, offering a compact platform for probing entanglement in non-Hermitian systems.|
|**2026-09-25**|**Entanglement area law in interacting bosons from the Bose-Hubbard model to $\phi$ 4 theory and beyond**|Donghoon Kim, Tomotaka Kuwahara|[[arxiv:2411.02157](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02157)]|The paper addresses the longstanding challenge of extending the entanglement area law to interacting boson systems with long-range interactions and unbounded local energy, where previous proofs did not apply. These results simultaneously resolve two major theoretical obstacles and provide a practical foundation for numerically simulating long-range cold atomic systems.|

These predictions are verified numerically in exactly solvable chiral-N Landau levels and in Schrödinger and Dirac moiré skyrmion crystals, with the integrated weight growing linearly and the resonant peak cubically for a Schrödinger flat band with a distorted skyrmion texture.
