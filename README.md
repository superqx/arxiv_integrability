|**2026-10-22**|**Generalized Coordinate Bethe Ansatz and Quantum Integrability of the Time-Dependent Massive Thirring Model**|Parameshwar R. Pasnoori|[[arxiv:2610.29998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29998)]|The paper addresses the challenge of constructing an exact quantum Bethe-ansatz framework for the massive Thirring model when the fermion mass is explicitly time-dependent, a setting where standard fixed-rapidity sectors fail to close under spatial winding. The authors develop a generalized coordinate Bethe ansatz acting directly on particle coordinates and physical rapidities, introducing a rapidity lattice generated by the finite shift β→β−p, with particle-exchange amplitudes governed by scalar analytic difference equations of Ruijsenaars type. Consistency of the construction singles out an exponential evolution of the bare mass, yielding a new realization of the time-dependent integrability–renormalization group correspondence, and the scheme reduces to conventional massive-Thirring Bethe equations in the static limit. Through the massive-Thirring/sine-Gordon equivalence, the work links this exact quantum construction to the generalized space–time-dependent Lax formalism, extending quantum integrability to time-dependent settings.|
|**2026-10-22**|**"Massive" Conformal Field Theories and application to Nambu-Goto string**|Yuri Makeenko|[[arxiv:2610.28888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28888)]|The paper addresses the apparent contradiction of two-dimensional theories with dimensionful parameters that nonetheless possess a traceless improved energy-momentum tensor and are conformally invariant. The author reviews progress on such "massive" conformal field theories, focusing on Liouville theory augmented by four-derivative terms that arise from the Nambu-Goto string and distinguish it from the Polyakov string. The exact solution in embedding dimension d=4 is shown to be consistent—unlike the Polyakov string—and is described by the (4,3) unitary minimal model. This clarifies the conformal structure of the Nambu-Goto string and its difference from the Polyakov formulation.|
|**2026-10-22**|**Orientifold and Integrability: $\mathcal{N}=4$ Super-Yang-Mills on $\mathbb{RP}^4$**|Joao Caetano, Shota Komatsu, Paolo Soresina, Anders Wallberg|[[arxiv:2610.28725](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28725)]|The paper investigates planar N=4 super Yang-Mills on ℝP⁴, addressing whether integrability survives the orientifold projection that combines the ℤ₂ involution with charge conjugation. Using integrability, holography, and supersymmetric localization, the authors compute one-point functions of non-BPS single-trace operators at weak coupling, map them to overlaps between energy eigenstates and crosscap states in integrable spin chains, and find a parity selection rule with results expressed as ratios of determinants. They conjecture a general closed formula and provide evidence for a holographic dual probe O1-plane in AdS₅×S⁵ via worldsheet crosscap states in the pp-wave limit. Exact finite-coupling results for half-BPS operators, obtained from a Wishart-Laguerre matrix integral, interpolate between weak-coupling gauge theory, strong-coupling supergravity, and the pp-wave description, establishing that integrability is preserved under the orientifold.|

Pasnoori|[[arxiv:2610.29998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29998)]|The paper addresses the challenge of solving the time-dependent massive Thirring model, where an explicitly time-varying fermion mass couples right- and left-moving modes and prevents fixed-rapidity sectors from closing under spatial winding. The authors develop a generalized coordinate Bethe ansatz that acts directly on particle coordinates and physical rapidities, introducing a rapidity lattice generated by finite shifts and deriving Ruijsenaars-type scalar analytic difference equations for particle exchange amplitudes. Consistency of this construction uniquely selects an exponential evolution of the bare mass, yielding a new realization of the time-dependent integrability-renormalization group correspondence. |**2026-10-22**|**Stabilizing and detecting electron fractionalization in the Hofstadter-Hubbard model**|Jiahao Yang, Zeyu Han, Gang v. Chen|[[arxiv:2610.29916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29916)]|The paper investigates electron fractionalization in the half-filled triangular-lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard model, motivated by correlated Chern insulator physics in moiré systems. The authors show that increasing orbital flux stabilizes and broadens the U(1)₂ chiral spin liquid phase in the Mott regime, where electrons fractionalize into gapped spinons and gapped chargons whose effective bands depend on the orbital flux. The convolution of narrow spinon bands with gapped chargon excitations produces rich, discernible structures in the electron spectral function and density of states. These findings identify orbital flux as a control parameter for fractionalization patterns and the Mott transition, and provide spectral signatures for ARPES and STM experiments linking free-particle band topology to intrinsic topological order.|
|**2026-10-22**|**Interaction-Induced Bloch Geometry and Transverse Thermoelectric Transport**|Heng-Yu Di, YuanDong Wang, Zhen-Gang Zhu, Gang Su|[[arxiv:2610.29486](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29486)]|The paper addresses how electron-electron interactions modify Bloch wavefunction geometry and the resulting linear thermoelectric response. Using a thermal length-gauge density-matrix approach within Hartree-Fock, the authors derive the response and separate it into Drude, symmetric quantum-metric, and antisymmetric Berry-curvature channels, decomposing the reconstructed Berry connection into noninteracting and interaction parts. They establish symmetry constraints on these channels and show that, under the relaxation-time approximation, the leading weak-broadening scaling is Γ⁻¹ for the Drude term, Γ for the quantum-metric term, and Γ⁰ for the Berry-curvature term. Self-consistent calculations for the triangular-lattice extended Hubbard model demonstrate that interactions can generate Berry curvature and a quantum metric, alter system symmetry, and thus drive transverse thermoelectric transport.|
|**2026-10-22**|**Chirality, Klein duality, and the stability of Kitaev spin liquids on the star lattice**|Harsh Raj, Yasir Iqbal|[[arxiv:2610.29278](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29278)]|The paper investigates why the exactly solvable Yao-Kivelson chiral Kitaev spin liquid on the star lattice shows a wide stability window under Heisenberg exchange for ferromagnetic Kitaev coupling but only a narrow one for antiferromagnetic coupling. Using projective symmetry groups (PSGs) of fermionic partons, the authors show that time-reversal symmetry must be broken for any Kitaev-type spin liquid on this lattice when spin-gauge locking holds, and that the Abelian and non-Abelian chiral liquids of both coupling signs share a single chiral PSG with a band-inversion transition between them. They further establish that Klein duality permutes the PSGs while leaving locked ones invariant, and via exact second-order perturbation theory they trace the ferromagnetic/antiferromagnetic asymmetry to constructive versus nearly destructive interference of two off-diagonal Heisenberg terms creating four visons on inter-triangle bonds. The work rests on a classification of 352 \(Z_2\) PSGs with spin-orbit-coupled amplitudes and also identifies fermionic partners of known Schwinger-boson spin liquids.|
|**2026-10-22**|**Pseudogap and Non-Fermi-liquid criticality in double Kondo model for bilayer nickelates**|Jing-Yu Zhao, Ya-Hui Zhang|[[arxiv:2603.25742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25742)]|The paper investigates the normal-state phase diagram of a bilayer Kondo lattice model, motivated by high-temperature superconductivity in bilayer nickelates, using single-site dynamical mean-field theory. In the absence of interlayer tunneling, the authors identify a non-Fermi-liquid critical point tuned by interlayer spin coupling or hole doping that separates a standard Fermi liquid from a pseudogap metal (the "second Fermi liquid") with small hole pockets and a violated perturbative Luttinger theorem. They provide an ancilla-fermion framework describing the pseudogap and spin-polaron physics, and extend the analysis to finite interlayer tunneling to address La₃Ni₂O₇. The work suggests that current nickelate samples near x ≈ 0.5 lie in the overdoped Fermi-liquid regime, with the pseudogap and non-Fermi-liquid criticality potentially accessible via electron doping.|

|**2026-10-22**|**From infinite symmetries to multi-soliton manifolds: dispersion-driven reduction for the KP and mKP equations**|Xiazhi Hao, Mingxia Ji, Senyue Lou|[[arxiv:2610.29770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29770)]|The paper studies how the infinite-dimensional symmetry algebras of the Kadomtsev–Petviashvili (KP) and modified KP equations behave when restricted to finite-dimensional multi-soliton manifolds. For KP, the authors analyze both the K-symmetry and τ-symmetry hierarchies, showing that each higher-order K-symmetry reduces to a linear combination of the n independent translational symmetries of the individual solitons, with coefficients given by dispersion relations at the soliton parameters; τ-symmetries reduce to combinations of three fundamental soliton symmetries with rational coefficients. Closed-form odd/even-order expressions and determinant representations are derived, and analogous reduction patterns are established for mKP. The work links infinite symmetry structure to soliton spectral data, yielding a unified interpretation in which higher-order symmetries generate weighted superpositions of elementary soliton translations.|
