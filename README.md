|**2026-08-26**|**New RG flows between non-Unitary CFTs from exact massless scattering theories**|Changrim Ahn, Zoltan Bajnok, Soma Elek|[[arxiv:2608.24385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24385)]|This paper introduces a new infinite family of integrable renormalization group (RG) flows between non-unitary conformal field theories (CFTs). The method involves constructing exact solutions to the massless S-matrix bootstrap equations, which describe the scattering of particles in these theories. Key results include the explicit construction of flows from higher-fusion-level minimal models to non-unitary Virasoro minimal models, verified through conformal perturbation theory and thermodynamic Bethe ansatz calculations, and the demonstration that non-invertible Verlinde defect lines are preserved along these flows. This work significantly expands the known landscape of solvable RG flows and may offer new insights into the structure of non-unitary CFTs and their deformations.|
|**2026-08-26**|**Heavenly equations in de Sitter space**|Maciej Dunajski, Timothy Moy|[[arxiv:2606.14572](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14572)]|This paper demonstrates a geometric unification in general relativity by proving that all anti-self-dual Einstein metrics with a non-zero cosmological constant can be locally derived from a single second-order partial differential equation, originally proposed by Lipstein and Nagy. The authors place this equation within the established "heavenly" formalism of Plebański and construct a corresponding Lax pair, indicating its integrable structure. A key result is the recovery of Plebański's second heavenly equation as the cosmological constant approaches zero, showing a smooth limit to the standard vacuum case. The work is significant because it provides a unifying framework for describing a class of gravitational instantons and clarifies the connection between cosmological and asymptotically flat heavenly equations.|
|**2026-08-26**|**A Note on the Perturbative Expansion of the Schwinger Model on $S^2$**|Joseph Smith|[[arxiv:2603.21938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21938)]|This paper examines the perturbative structure of the Schwinger model, a solvable quantum field theory, when defined on a two-dimensional sphere. The authors conduct a perturbative analysis to test whether its quantum corrections align with predictions derived from the model's exact solution. Their findings provide evidence that the perturbative expansion on the sphere is consistent with the exact results. This work confirms the reliability of perturbative methods for this model on curved spacetime and supports the broader understanding of its non-trivial dynamics.|

|**2026-08-26**|**Accidental accuracy and vertex corrections in $GW$: Exact benchmarks for the extended Hubbard model**|Michael O. Atambo|[[arxiv:2608.24838](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24838)]|The paper addresses the poorly quantified accuracy of the widely used $GW$ approximation in strongly correlated systems, where vertex corrections are typically neglected. Using exact diagonalization of the extended Hubbard model as a benchmark, the authors construct a model-space $GW$ theory to quantify its errors across interaction strengths. Key results reveal that vertex corrections change character from suppressing fluctuations at weak coupling to enhancing them in the Mott regime, and that including them in the polarizability channel actually worsens gap predictions. The study also identifies an accidental exactness of static $COHSEX$ at a specific interaction strength, offering valuable quantitative diagnostics for assessing $GW$ reliability in correlated materials.|
|**2026-08-26**|**Transport interpretation of entanglement Hamiltonian cumulants in integrable quantum quenches**|Riccardo Travaglino, Pasquale Calabrese|[[arxiv:2608.24613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24613)]|This paper investigates the dynamics of entanglement Hamiltonian cumulants in interacting integrable models after global quantum quenches. The authors show that these cumulants can be exactly expressed as current cumulants of conserved charges in the post-quench macrostate, establishing a direct link between entanglement evolution and transport. In free and conformal limits, the results match known expressions involving left- and right-moving excitations, while in interacting models a similar structure persists in the entanglement spectrum. This provides a unified transport-based framework for understanding entanglement fluctuations across diverse integrable systems, complementing the quasiparticle picture for entropy growth.|
|**2026-08-26**|**Dynamic quantum phase transitions in the two-leg Creutz ladder with long-range hopping**|J. A. da Silva, J. C. Xavier|[[arxiv:2608.24514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24514)]|This paper investigates dynamic quantum phase transitions in a two-leg Creutz ladder model that includes long-range hopping with power-law decay and finite range. The authors develop an exact solution for a generic two-band model, which allows them to compute the Loschmidt amplitude and identify non-analyticities in the dynamical free energy at critical times. They find that the number and density of these critical times depend on the hopping exponent and range, with small exponents and large ranges producing increasingly dense non-analyticities. This work extends the understanding of dynamical phase transitions to long-range interacting systems, demonstrating behavior analogous to that previously found in the SSH model.|
|**2026-08-26**|**Altermagnetic Anomalous Hall Effect and Spin--Edge-Locked Chiral Modes in a Modified Kane--Mele--Hubbard Model**|Mohsen Hafez-Torbati, Alireza Qaiumzadeh|[[arxiv:2608.24408](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24408)]|This paper introduces a new mechanism for achieving the altermagnetic anomalous Hall effect (AHE) in a modified Kane-Mele-Hubbard model. Using dynamical mean-field theory, the authors show that hole doping drives a spin-flop transition to an out-of-plane antiferromagnetic phase, which breaks a key symmetry and generates a finite AHE. Notably, this AHE receives equal contributions from both spin sectors and is accompanied by spin-edge-locked chiral states, distinguishing it from the conventional ferromagnetic Hall effect. The work is significant as it demonstrates that the altermagnetic AHE can be realized simply through carrier doping and Hubbard interactions, offering a realistic path for its observation in correlated transition metal dichalcogenides.|
|**2026-08-26**|**Influence of the inter-orbital interaction and kinetic terms on superconductivity: a simple two-orbital Hubbard model**|Vito Marino, Diego Florez-Ablan, Luca F. Tocchio, Massimo Capone, Federico Becca|[[arxiv:2608.24337](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24337)]|This paper uses quantum Monte Carlo simulations on a two-orbital Hubbard model to study how inter-orbital interactions and hopping affect superconductivity. The method reveals that adding inter-orbital repulsion or hopping significantly enhances superconducting correlations compared to a single-orbital system. At a strong interaction strength, the inter-orbital repulsion effectively screens the intra-orbital repulsion, shifting the superconducting dome, while additional inter-orbital hopping further boosts pairing by creating both flat and broad electronic bands. The findings highlight a purely electronic mechanism where multi-orbital effects can strongly promote superconductivity.|
|**2026-08-26**|**Collective photon echoes in the Tavis-Cummings model: distribution independence, two detuning regimes, and the Dicke ladder**|Michael Tavis|[[arxiv:2608.21442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21442)]|This paper presents an exact solution for collective photon echoes in the Tavis-Cummings model, showing that an initially unexcited ensemble of molecules in a lossless cavity produces periodic intensity recurrences. The echo time is confirmed to scale as \(4\pi\sqrt{N+\Delta}/g\) and is shown to be independent of the initial photon statistics, though the echo amplitude depends on the distribution shape and squeezing phase. The study also reveals two distinct detuning regimes and shows that the echo envelope structure changes based on the initial Dicke state, with single-component echoes occurring only from the ground state. The results are significant for quantum information and cavity QED experiments, as they demonstrate the feasibility of observing these echoes on existing five-qubit superconducting hardware, with the signal surviving realistic decoherence channels.|
|**2026-08-26**|**Phase diagram of the Kitaev-Heisenberg- $\Gamma$ model: Classical and quantum magnetism, frustration, and subdominant interactions**|Kiyu Fukui, Yukitoshi Motome|[[arxiv:2606.13263](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13263)]|This paper revisits the ground-state phase diagram of the Kitaev-Heisenberg-Gamma model, a framework for describing candidate Kitaev spin liquid materials, using both classical and quantum numerical methods. The study identifies a diverse array of noncollinear multiple-Q magnetic orders in the classical limit, but finds that quantum fluctuations suppress many of these, leaving a smaller set of dominant incommensurate orders. The authors pinpoint highly frustrated regions where additional magnetic interactions could stabilize spiral spin liquids or new ordered states. These findings offer a comprehensive guide for interpreting experiments on existing materials and for designing new ones that might realize a Kitaev spin liquid.|
|**2026-08-26**|**Inverted duality of Hubbard model and an equation for the Green's function**|Xiao-Yong Feng|[[arxiv:2409.09562](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09562)]|This paper introduces a novel mathematical perspective on the Hubbard model, a fundamental framework for studying strongly correlated electrons. The authors identify an inherent "inverted duality" property within the model's structure, which they leverage to derive a new equation that the electron Green's function must satisfy. This result offers a fresh, potentially exact tool for analyzing the model, promising deeper insights into electron correlations and phase transitions. The work is significant as it opens a new avenue for theoretical exploration of a central problem in condensed matter physics.|

The method involves applying a reciprocal transformation to convert the extended mKdV into a new nonlinear evolution equation with a source term, followed by an Ermakov-Painlevé II symmetry reduction to find exact solutions. The key result is the derivation of exact analytical solutions for these Stefan-type problems, which admit moving boundaries. The authors connect this equation to Plebański’s heavenly formalism and provide a Lax pair, indicating its integrability. They also demonstrate that Plebański's second heavenly equation emerges as the cosmological constant approaches zero. The work thus unifies previously separate descriptions of these geometric structures, offering a new tool for studying gravitational instantons and integrable systems.|
