|**2026-10-09**|**Mass quench in interacting finite-size quantum field system**|Grachik A Simonian, Alexey G. Mikhaylenko, Andrew G. Semenov|[[arxiv:2610.12045](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12045)]|The paper studies how correlation functions of an interacting quantum scalar field evolve out of equilibrium after a sudden mass quench in a finite-size system. The authors numerically solve the one-loop self-consistent Keldysh equation. They find that at a time roughly half the system size, the effective mass is strongly disturbed due to interference between counter-propagating fluctuation wavefronts inside the light cone, an effect caused by periodic boundary conditions. This reveals a distinct finite-size spatiotemporal signature in quantum quench dynamics.|
|**2026-10-09**|**Nonreciprocal black holes with primary hair in beyond Horndeski gravity: Exact solutions and stability**|Sebastian Bahamonde, Theodoros Nakas, Simon Iteanu|[[arxiv:2610.11712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11712)]|The paper addresses the construction of exact, asymptotically flat black-hole solutions in shift- and \(Z_2\)-symmetric beyond Horndeski theories, relaxing the restrictive reciprocal condition \(g_{tt}g_{rr}=-1\) that limited earlier work. The authors develop a systematic method that yields two branches of hairy solutions—one with khronometric hair and trivial Noether current, the other with genuine primary scalar hair and a nonvanishing propagating degree of freedom. They derive several families of exact solutions, show that the generally singular primary-hair solutions can be regularized into regular black holes or solitons with secondary hair, and find evidence for linear stability under axial and monopole even-parity perturbations. These results expand the space of viable hairy black holes in modified gravity and clarify the distinction between primary and secondary scalar hair in beyond Horndeski theories.|
|**2026-10-09**|**Pre-geometric Black Holes with Spherical Symmetry**|Giuseppe Meluccio, Egor Pluzhnikov|[[arxiv:2610.11357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11357)]|The paper addresses classical black hole singularities by studying spherically symmetric pre-geometric black hole configurations, in which gravity emerges as a low-energy effective theory via spontaneous symmetry breaking of an underlying non-metric spacetime. Using the correspondence principle, the authors derive exact vacuum solutions for different pre-geometric theories that serve as analogues of the (anti-)de Sitter-Schwarzschild metric. One solution contains a Planck-sized (anti-)de Sitter core, and the work is supplemented by an analysis of a pre-geometric version of Birkhoff's theorem. These results suggest how conventional black hole geometry may emerge from more fundamental pre-geometric structures and offer a possible route to singularity avoidance at the Planck scale.|
|**2026-10-09**|**Fourier analysis of compact oscillons**|Pawel Klimas, Leandro Roza Livramento, Jo\~ao Saldanha Streibel|[[arxiv:2610.10746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10746)]|The paper derives the exact compact oscillon in the signum-Gordon model using Fourier methods, expressing it as a uniformly convergent series of a fundamental frequency and its odd harmonics. This formulation avoids the convergence failure of standard perturbative series for analytic potentials and circumvents the non-existence theorem for localized, small-amplitude periodic states. The authors find that the fundamental mode alone approximates the exact solution remarkably well, with accuracy improving when the first two non-vanishing harmonics are added, and they generalize the framework to a swaying compact oscillon with a periodically moving boundary. The swaying motion arises from even-order modes coupling to odd source terms, producing dynamic modulation despite time-independent spatial Fourier profiles.|
|**2026-10-09**|**Generalised Gibbs Ensembles from the ODE/IM Correspondence: the Lee--Yang Model**|Sujay K. Chemical potentials are encoded as deformations of the cubic oscillator, fixed order by order so that the large-energy WKB period reproduces the driving term of the corresponding thermodynamic Bethe ansatz equation, with subleading periods predicting deformed conserved-charge eigenvalues. Working out the spin-5 ensemble for the ground state and the identity-module primary, the authors find that the Stokes multiplier obeys the Lee–Yang Y-system and that deformed eigenvalues agree with independent conformal-field-theory calculations. Borel resummation of the ground-state energy series matches numerical TBA results over many orders of magnitude, and the approach is expected to generalize to other integrable models.|
|**2026-10-09**|**Gluons and Knots**|Alfredo Guevara|[[arxiv:2610.10673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10673)]|The paper shows that the scattering amplitude of one negative-helicity gluon with many positive-helicity gluons has a topological structure, remaining constant within kinematic chambers and factorizing through wall-crossing. Using Yangian invariance, the amplitude is mapped to a 2D null Wilson loop that lifts to a positive transverse 3D link, allowing wall-crossing and the soft theorem to be recast as skein relations and Reidemeister moves on subknots. The resulting state-counting description adds corners of HOMFLY-PT polynomials and yields Catalan numbers in special "star" kinematics, matching knot theory results. This establishes a precise conjectured link between gluon amplitudes and topological field/string theory, with a Lean-formalized derivation in a companion paper.|
|**2026-10-09**|**Asymptotically Solvable Quantum Circuits**|Samuel H. Pickering, Bruno Bertini|[[arxiv:2602.24276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24276)]|The paper addresses whether generic, chaotic quantum dynamics can be understood despite being generally intractable, given that exactly solvable circuits impose nontrivial constraints on correlations. It introduces a family of "asymptotically solvable" quantum circuits in which solvability constraints only affect correlations beyond a tuneable length scale, so the dynamics are exact only at sufficiently long times while remaining generic at short times. Using both analytical results from a non-interacting point and numerical experiments, the authors compute equilibrium dynamical correlations and thermalization dynamics after quenches from compatible initial states. This provides a framework for interpolating between generic and solvable behavior, offering a rare window into the early-time dynamics of ergodic quantum systems.|
|**2026-10-09**|**From Bertotti--Robinson to Vacuum: New Exact Solutions in General Relativity via Harrison and Inversion Symmetries**|Jos\'e Barrientos, Adolfo Cisterna, Amaro D\'iaz, Keanu M\"uller|[[arxiv:2602.17581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17581)]|Starting from accelerating Bertotti–Robinson black holes embedded in external electromagnetic backgrounds, the authors apply two independent electrovacuum symmetries—a Melvin–Bonnor-type magnetization and a magnetic inversion—to generate new vacuum solutions of the Einstein equations. In both constructions the external electromagnetic field can be switched off while leaving a nontrivial gravitational backreaction, producing accelerating Petrov type I vacuum spacetimes; the static limit recovers known magnetized Bertotti–Robinson–Schwarzschild results, whereas inversion yields a genuinely new two-parameter extension of the Schwarzschild–Levi-Civita geometry. They further give a stationary generalization and two new static vacuum configurations from an Alekseev–García seed, and analyze the main geometrical properties.
