|**2026-08-20**|**Spin precession and anomalous dipole couplings in a plane-wave Yang-Mills background**|V. V. Parazian|[[arxiv:2608.18817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18817)]|This paper addresses the problem of fermion spin dynamics and dipole interactions in a non-Abelian Yang-Mills plane-wave background. The method employs exact solutions of the Dirac equation, the exact fermion Green's function, and a renormalized one-loop vertex in an axial gauge to derive spin-dependent structures beyond the tree level. Key results include a non-Abelian extension of the Bargmann-Michel-Telegdi equation, a phase-dependent Pauli form factor acting as an anomalous chromomagnetic dipole, and dynamically induced spin-precession axes from the field commutator, leading to coupled spin-color precession. The significance lies in providing explicit weak-field and harmonic-expansion forms for spin-flip probabilities and asymmetries, directly linking background-field propagators to observable effects in strong gauge fields.|
|**2026-08-20**|**A more general exact solution for the Schwarzschild black hole in a Bertotti-Robinson-Bonnor-Melvin universe**|Andrea Di Pinto|[[arxiv:2608.18213](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18213)]|The paper addresses problems in existing exact solutions for black holes in external electromagnetic fields, specifically cases with negative mass and thermodynamic inconsistencies. The authors introduce a new parameterization for the Ovcharenko-Podolský family and then generalize it via a Harrison transformation to include a Bonnor-Melvin external field. This yields a new family of Einstein-Maxwell solutions that maintain positive mass and satisfy black hole thermodynamics after fixing gauge constants. The work's significance lies in providing a more general, physically consistent framework for such black hole solutions, with a vacuum subcase connected to prior metrics.|

## Mathematical Physics

|Publish Date|Title|Authors|arXiv|Summary|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**2026-08-20**|**Gaudin models for some classical multivariate distributions**|Plamen Iliev|[[arxiv:2608.18930](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18930)]|This paper studies Gaudin models linked to classical multivariate probability distributions, specifically Hahn, Dirichlet, multinomial, and negative multinomial. The authors use representation theory to build multivariate orthogonal polynomials as common eigenfunctions of Gaudin operators. These polynomials are parameterized by solutions to Bethe ansatz equations or by roots of Heine-Stieltjes polynomials. This work connects algebraic structures to probability theory, providing a new framework for constructing orthogonal polynomials tied to these distributions.|
|**2026-08-20**|**Near-critical Ising, sine-Gordon at the free fermion point, and bosonization**|S. C. Park, Tuomas Virtanen, Christian Webb|[[arxiv:2512.11304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11304)]|This paper investigates the continuous correlation functions of the two-dimensional near-critical Ising model with plus boundary conditions. The authors prove that doubled correlations of primary fields match those of the sine-Gordon model at the free fermion point, providing a concrete instance of bosonization. The proof relies on analyticity in the mass parameter and matching Taylor coefficients, using precise estimates for massive holomorphic functions on the Ising side and a Brydges–Kennedy-style Mayer expansion on the sine-Gordon side. This result rigorously establishes a key link between massive statistical mechanics models, deepening the understanding of bosonization beyond criticality.|

## Condensed Matter

|Publish Date|Title|Authors|arXiv|Summary|

|**2026-08-20**|**Chiral bosonic mean-field Ansatz and spin dynamics in spin-1 Kitaev magnets**|Daiki Sasamoto, Arnaud Ralko, Jaime Merino, Joji Nasu|[[arxiv:2608.19178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19178)]|This paper addresses the challenge of understanding spin dynamics in higher-spin Kitaev models, which are not exactly solvable. The authors use a Schwinger-boson mean-field theory to compare the triplet 0-flux and π/2-flux Ansätze for the antiferromagnetic S=1 Kitaev model, calculating the dynamical spin structure factor and real-space correlations. They find that the π/2-flux Ansatz produces a flatter spectrum and more strongly suppressed long-range correlations, linking these features to short-range spin order. The study further shows that this chiral Ansatz remains valid for weak off-diagonal exchanges, matching exact diagonalization results and supporting its use as a mean-field description near the Kitaev limit.|
|**2026-08-20**|**Sign-problem-resilient singular-value probe in determinant quantum Monte Carlo**|Wen Chen, Rubem Mondaini|[[arxiv:2608.19028](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19028)]|This paper addresses the sign problem in determinant quantum Monte Carlo (DQMC) simulations of fermionic systems. The authors propose a new probe, based on the adjacent gap ratio of low-lying singular values of the space-time fermion matrix, to detect interaction-driven phase transitions. They test this method on two honeycomb-lattice Hubbard models, finding it accurately tracks the known transition in the sign-free model and remains effective in the sign-problematic Haldane-Hubbard model, even when phase reweighting is weak. The results demonstrate that singular-value statistics provide a robust, sign-problem-resilient tool for identifying quantum phase transitions in DQMC.|
|**2026-08-20**|**Hubb_DMFT and Wan2mb_DMFT: Continuous-Time Quantum Monte Carlo Solvers for Single- and Multi-Orbital Hubbard Model**|A. A. Katanin|[[arxiv:2608.18540](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18540)]|These software packages provide numerical solutions for strongly correlated electron systems using Dynamical Mean-Field Theory (DMFT). Hubb_DMFT is tailored for single-band Hubbard models, calculating self-energies and two-particle vertices, while Wan2mb_DMFT extends these capabilities to realistic multi-orbital systems by interfacing with Wannier tight-binding Hamiltonians. Both solvers use a modified Continuous-Time Quantum Monte Carlo core with hybridisation expansion and improved estimators to achieve numerically exact results. The significance lies in offering versatile, ready-to-use tools that bridge model-based and material-specific DMFT calculations.|
|**2026-08-20**|**Embedding Paired Free-Fermion Gaussian States into Gutzwiller-Projected Bardeen--Cooper--Schrieffer Wave Functions**|Ryui Kaneko|[[arxiv:2608.18168](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18168)]|This paper addresses the challenge of constructing suitable initial states for variational Monte Carlo simulations of nonintegrable quantum models. The authors show that any even-parity free-fermion Gaussian state can be exactly represented as a Gutzwiller-projected BCS wave function of spinful Abrikosov fermions, using the one-dimensional transverse-field Ising model as a demonstration. The key method involves embedding the fermionic state into a paired exponential form within the projected BCS framework. This construction provides a systematic and controlled way to generate initial states, which is significant for improving the efficiency and accuracy of variational Monte Carlo studies.|
|**2026-08-20**|**Integer-Graded Reciprocal Skin Effect from Internal Rotation**|Kin Hung Fung, C. T. Chan|[[arxiv:2608.01520](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01520)]|This paper investigates non-Hermitian skin effects, where a system's modes localize at boundaries. The authors discover that conserved internal rotation generalizes the known binary (Z₂) classification of such effects into an unbounded integer-graded hierarchy, with each angular momentum channel behaving as an independent asymmetric chain. They show that half-integer spin systems realize the symplectic class, with the grading parity recovering the known Z₂ index, while mixing between conjugate channels destroys the structure. The findings, demonstrated in minimal lattice and resonator models, significantly expand the theoretical framework for classifying and realizing reciprocal skin effects.|
|**2026-08-20**|**Bosonic Working Media in a Frustrated Rhombi Chain: Otto and Stirling Cycles from Flat Bands, Caging, and Flux Control**|Francisco J. Pe\~na, Rafael Garc\'ia-Zamora, Gabriele De Chiara, Jorge Flores, Santiago Henr\'iquez, Nathan M. Myers, Felipe Barra, Patricio Vargas|[[arxiv:2604.13185](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13185)]|This paper explores using flat-band engineering to enhance the performance of quantum heat engines. The authors model noninteracting bosons on a rhombic chain with a tunable magnetic flux, which reshapes the energy spectrum from dispersive to flat bands in the caging regime. They find that the Otto cycle's work output and efficiency improve significantly near this regime due to suppressed heat released to the cold reservoir, whereas the Stirling cycle achieves broader work extraction but lower efficiency via flux-driven entropy changes. The results establish geometric frustration and flux control as practical thermodynamic resources for optimizing bosonic thermal machines.|
|**2026-08-20**|**A Correlated Route to Antiferromagnetic Spintronics**|Joel Bobadilla, Alberto Camjayi|[[arxiv:2603.16552](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16552)]|This paper introduces a symmetry selection rule that enables spin-polarized charge currents in collinear antiferromagnets, overcoming their traditional passive role in spintronics. Using dynamical mean-field theory on the doped Hubbard model, the authors show that simultaneously breaking particle-hole symmetry and magnetic sublattice equivalence—via doping and a magnetic field—yields a sizable, tunable current polarization. The polarization peaks deep in the ordered phase and reverses sign at a nontrivial compensation point, with a structurally analogous mechanism in altermagnets. The findings establish electronic correlations as a key ingredient for active spintronic functionality in conventional antiferromagnets.|
