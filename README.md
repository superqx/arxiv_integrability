|**2026-10-12**|**Modular Properties of Generalised Gibbs Ensembles: The Deformed KdV Hierarchy**|Faisal Karimi|[[arxiv:2610.13875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13875)]|The paper addresses how modular transformations of Generalized Gibbs Ensembles (GGEs) with inserted KdV charges can be understood through deformed conserved charges. The authors construct these charges as formal power series in a chemical potential, with coefficients given by local integrals of motion, and prove their commutativity, uniqueness, and consistency via recursion relations and flow equations. They verify their construction using the Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz in two models: the \(c=1/2\) free fermion theory, where exact agreement with undeformed KdV charges is found, and the Lee-Yang model, where numerical agreement holds through cubic order in the spin-5 deformation. This work extends the theory of integrable deformations and provides a systematic framework for computing modular properties of GGEs in integrable quantum field theories.|
|**2026-10-12**|**The Quasiparticle Ansatz for Relativistic Continuous Matrix Product States**|Silviu S. Pufu, Benjamin T. S{\o}gaard, Spyros Terlemes|[[arxiv:2610.13824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13824)]|The paper addresses the lack of a systematic variational method for describing low-lying excitations and solitons in relativistic quantum field theories directly in the continuum. It introduces the Relativistic Quasiparticle (RQP) ansatz, which extends the Relativistic Continuous Matrix Product State vacuum by constructing one-particle excitations and solitons whose energies emerge as eigenvalues of an effective Hamiltonian. Applied to the sine-Gordon model, the ansatz reproduces integrability results for soliton and breather masses, and applied to \(\phi^4\) theory in both its \(\mathbb Z_2\)-preserving and broken phases, it yields the particle and soliton spectra, identifying where multiple stable particles exist. This provides a controlled tensor-network tool for computing excitation spectra in continuum relativistic QFTs.|
|**2026-10-12**|**Scalene Yang-Baxter triples realize all nearest-neighbour Hamiltonians with global additive symmetries**|Pramod Padmanabhan, Somnath Maity, Akash Sinha, Vladimir Korepin|[[arxiv:2610.13782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13782)]|The paper addresses the characterization of one-dimensional nearest-neighbour Hamiltonians that conserve a uniform or staggered sum of on-site operators \(w_j\). It proves that a Hamiltonian has such an additive symmetry if and only if its bond densities lie in the commutant of \(w_j \pm w_{j+1}\), and shows these Hamiltonians and their charges can be constructed from scalene Yang-Baxter triples via monodromy matrices. The construction works for arbitrary finite local dimension, periodic chains of length \(L \geq 3\), and without translation invariance or ordinary Yang-Baxter integrability, exhausting all Hamiltonians with the prescribed additive symmetry. This provides a route to classifying such Hamiltonians by classifying one-site generators and their bond commutants, with illustrations for spin-\(\tfrac12\) and spin-1 systems.|
|**2026-10-12**|**Robustness of exact $SU(N)$ Einstein-Skyrme solutions: black holes, wormholes and cosmologies**|Carla Henr\'iquez-Baez, Perla Medina, Pablo Rodr\'iguez, Aldo Vera|[[arxiv:2610.13566](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13566)]|The paper investigates whether exact gravitational solutions—black holes, wormholes, and cosmologies—found in the $SU(2)$ Einstein-Skyrme model survive generalization to $SU(N)$ with arbitrary flavor number. Using analytic methods, it shows that key physical properties (thermodynamics, baryon number, geometry) depend on both the number of flavors and the coupling constants. Increasing matter content enhances the local thermal stability of spherically symmetric black holes, slows cosmic scale-factor growth while raising the Hubble parameter, and enlarges traversable wormhole throats because the required energy density saturates at a finite value. These results establish that such configurations are robust under flavor extension and reveal how flavor number can be used to tune gravitational properties.|
|**2026-10-12**|**Tropical QFT and the sine-Gordon model**|Michael Borinsky, Jury Radkovski, Suriyah Rajalingam Kannagi|[[arxiv:2610.12653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12653)]|Tropical quantum field theory is proposed as an exactly solvable deformation limit of ordinary QFT governed by a nonlinear PDE, and the two-dimensional sine-Gordon model is used as a test case to compare deformed and undeformed features. The authors classify scale-invariant tropical QFTs in D=2 and use functional renormalization group methods to show that all scale-invariant theories with periodic effective actions arise as infrared fixed points of flows from sine-Gordon Lagrangians. For the tropical sine-Gordon model they compute the beta functions exactly, solve the renormalized theory both by a convergent expansion and numerically for all admissible couplings, and trace the full RG flow from the Gaussian to the IR fixed point. They also link the large-order behavior of the loop expansion to the Hamilton-Jacobi equation, interpreting its solutions as tropical instantons, thereby establishing tropical QFT as a tractable framework for studying integrable models.|
