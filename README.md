|**2026-10-23**|**Sign-problem-free Bosonic Auxiliary Field Quantum Monte Carlo**|Yin-Kai Yu, Zhong-Chao Wei, Zi-Xiang Li|[[arxiv:2610.31541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31541)]|The paper addresses the sign problem, a fundamental barrier to exact quantum Monte Carlo simulations of interacting bosons, which is especially severe on geometrically frustrated lattices. The authors develop a bosonic auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo (BAFQMC) method and rigorously prove that either time-reversal symmetry or reflection positivity provides a general sufficient condition for sign-problem-free simulations. Benchmarked on a two-flavor Bose-Hubbard model on the triangular lattice, BAFQMC shows excellent quantitative agreement with finite-temperature exact diagonalization while evading the sign problem where conventional world-line and stochastic-series-expansion methods fail. These unifying principles substantially expand the reach of unbiased, numerically exact simulations for frustrated bosonic matter.|
|**2026-10-23**|**An elliptic generalization of the $D^{(2)}_{2}$ Spin chain**|Yi Qiao, Pei Sun, Xiaotian Xu, Junpeng Cao, Guang-Liang Li, Wen-Li Yang, Kangjie Shi, Yupeng Wang|[[arxiv:2610.31381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31381)]|The paper constructs an integrable elliptic generalization of the closed \(D^{(2)}_2\) quantum spin chain, addressing the lack of an elliptic deformation for this model. The method uses an R-matrix that reduces, via a similarity transformation, to the known \(D^{(2)}_2\) trigonometric R-matrix in the trigonometric limit, with integrability established through factorization of the R-matrix and transfer matrix. The authors derive an exact solution expressed as a homogeneous T-Q relation. This extends the known integrable structure of the \(D^{(2)}_2\) chain to the elliptic setting and provides its exact solution.|
|**2026-10-23**|**Explicit quasi-periodic solutions to the discrete derivative nonlinear Schr\"odinger equation**|Xiaoxue Xu, Wei Liu, Junyi Zhu|[[arxiv:2610.31347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31347)]|The paper addresses the construction of explicit quasi-periodic solutions for the discrete derivative nonlinear Schrödinger equation, a problem not fully resolved by existing approaches. The authors derive a Lax pair and use it to obtain a nonlinear integrable symplectic map, through which they compute Riemann theta function representations of the discrete potentials. This yields explicit solutions whose quasi-periodic character is demonstrated for genus-one and genus-two cases. The work provides a systematic route to quasi-periodic solutions of this discrete integrable system and illustrates their structure through low-genus examples.|
|**2026-10-23**|**First-order differential constraints for the nonlinear wave equation:wave fronts, admissible nonlinearities and exact solutions**|Oleg Kaptsov|[[arxiv:2610.30629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30629)]|The paper addresses the construction of exact solutions to the nonlinear wave equation in three spatial dimensions, a problem where symmetry-based methods often fail to capture generic behavior. The authors employ linear determining equations to derive first-order differential constraints, integrate these constraints, and substitute the resulting ansatz into the wave equation, yielding solutions that generically possess no Lie point symmetry. Key results include a classification linking admissible nonlinearities to constraint geometry—arbitrary nonlinearity on characteristic hyperplanes, cubic on light cones, and exponential or quintic on certain fronts—along with explicit solutions such as smooth bounded sine-Gordon walls, vortex configurations, fields of arbitrarily moving point sources, an exact nonlinear analogue of the Larmor formula, and finite-energy solutions with prescribed toroidal nodal surfaces. These findings significantly extend the solution landscape of nonlinear wave equations beyond symmetry-reduction techniques and provide physically relevant exact configurations, including radiating source analogues.|
