Zevola|[[arxiv:2608.06071](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06071)]|This paper introduces a new analytic method for studying multi-component cosmologies by reformulating the dynamics through a single scalar field whose potential is expressed via the Hubble function, treated as a "fake superpotential." This approach reduces the equations of motion to a first-order system, making it exactly solvable in a suitable time coordinate, and it provides a unified framework for standard cosmology, inflation, and dark energy. The authors construct specific integrable inflationary models and analyze their scalar and tensor perturbations by solving the Mukhanov-Sasaki equations beyond the slow-roll approximation. Their results yield predictions for the power spectrum, spectral indices, and tensor-to-scalar ratio that match current observational constraints, demonstrating that the method offers a powerful alternative to slow-roll approximations for testing inflationary theories.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Partition Functions of Hermitian and PT-Symmetric Oscillators from Integrable Models**|Hongfei Shu, Jingjing Yang|[[arxiv:2608.06047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06047)]|This paper presents a new method for calculating thermal partition functions and spectral zeta functions for homogeneous Hermitian and PT-symmetric oscillators. The approach uses an ODE/IM correspondence, where the quantum system's quantization condition is linked to the Destri-de Vega equation of an integrable model. By expressing these spectral functions as contour integrals of a counting function, the authors establish a direct connection between quantum observables and integrable systems. This work offers a novel and unified framework for analyzing the spectral properties of both Hermitian and non-Hermitian quantum systems.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Exact spectrum and anomalous relaxation in the open disorder-free Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev system**|Soshun Ozaki, Hironobu Yoshida, Hosho Katsura|[[arxiv:2606.08079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08079)]|This paper investigates a disorder-free version of the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (SYK) model that is coupled to an environment, using the Lindblad master equation. By exploiting the model's integrability, the authors derive an exact solution for the Liouvillian spectrum, providing the eigenvalues and projection operators for any system size. A key finding is that the Liouvillian gap, which sets the relaxation rate, remains non-zero even when dissipation is turned off in the thermodynamic limit, a phenomenon termed anomalous relaxation. This unexpected behavior, alongside other spectral features like complex-to-real eigenvalue transitions, suggests the model is a valuable platform for studying non-trivial open quantum dynamics.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Full Eigenstate Thermalization in Integrable Spin Systems**|Tanay Pathak|[[arxiv:2510.05887](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05887)]|This paper investigates the Eigenstate Thermalization Hypothesis (ETH) and its generalization, "full ETH," within integrable spin systems. Using exact diagonalization of the Ising and XXZ Heisenberg models, the authors numerically test full ETH predictions and contrast the results with chaotic systems. They find that while a fourth-order ETH free cumulant dominates the late-time behavior of the out-of-time-ordered correlator (OTOC), its dynamics deviate significantly from nonintegrable cases. The work highlights key differences in thermalization behavior between integrable and chaotic systems, extending the understanding of full ETH beyond its typical chaotic context.|

The solution reveals flux-shifted conformal towers in the gapless regime, demonstrating a direct link between parastatistics and conformal field theory.|

|**2026-08-07**|**Resilient strange metal at an unconventional quantum critical point in $d=2$**|J\'er\^ome Leblanc, A. -M. S. Tremblay|[[arxiv:2608.05988](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05988)]|This paper theoretically investigates strange-metal behavior in the two-dimensional Hubbard model near a quantum critical point. Using a non-perturbative improved two-particle self-consistent approach, the authors study the transition between a Fermi liquid and spin-density wave order for moderate interaction strengths. They find that for a wide range of correlation lengths, the system exhibits unconventional critical exponents, a strongly momentum-dependent self-energy, and no clear Landau quasiparticles. Despite this, the resistivity scales linearly with temperature, demonstrating that this classic strange-metal signature can exist without a linear-in-temperature self-energy, a key assumption in many theoretical models.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Local Spin Excitations Mediate Quasiparticle Breakdown in the Orbital-Selective Mott Phase**|Yuekun Niu, Yu Ni, Jia-Ming Wang, Zhong-Yi Lu, Yun Song, Shiping Feng|[[arxiv:2608.05556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05556)]|This paper investigates why quasiparticles break down in the orbital-selective Mott phase (OSMP), a state where localized and itinerant electrons coexist. Using dynamical mean-field theory on a two-band Hubbard model, the authors identify the spin-flip and Ising components of Hund's coupling as the origin. These terms create local spin excitations that couple the orbitals, renormalizing quasiparticle properties and destroying their coherence. The findings establish local spin excitations as the fundamental mechanism for quasiparticle breakdown, challenging the picture of effectively decoupled orbitals in the OSMP.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Morphology of frozen labyrinths from irreversible threshold dynamics**|Daniel Richard Levy (Princeton University)|[[arxiv:2608.05496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05496)]|This paper investigates irreversible majority threshold dynamics, where agents can flip their state only once, a rule that freezes balanced non-consensus labyrinths from random initial conditions. Using a Gaussian kernel on a lattice, the authors show that this one-flip rule arrests coarsening, producing bicontinuous patterns with a sublinear feature width that narrows as the interaction range grows. By intervening—fixing the initial condition while varying update order—they find the coarse domain layout is deterministic, with update order only fine-tuning wall positions through a secondary race. The frozen walls are marked by frustration, a signature absent in reversible relaxation, which carries little of the pattern's large-scale shape, revealing the mechanism of arrested coarsening.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Exact statistical transmutation of quantum mixtures on a ring**|Wayne J. The authors use an exact solution to show that, in the single-impurity limit on a ring with an artificial gauge field, both Fermi-Fermi and Bose-Bose mixtures exhibit exact anyonic exchange statistics between the impurity and majority particles. The resulting fractional phase is determined by the angular momentum sector, and the impurity's momentum distribution matches that of an ideal anyon, with its high-momentum tails revealing the statistical angle. The study also proposes a quench protocol for reversible dynamical anyonization, offering a concrete experimental path to create and manipulate anyonic states in ultracold atom systems.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Lorentz-Covariant Landau Levels of Tilted Dirac Fermions in Nonuniform Fields**|Sushmita Saha, Alestin Mawrie|[[arxiv:2607.17676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17676)]|This paper introduces an analytical framework for calculating Landau levels in tilted, anisotropic Dirac materials subjected to an exponentially decaying magnetic field, a problem lacking exact solutions in the lab frame. By applying scaling and a Lorentz boost to map the system to an equivalent isotropic Dirac equation in a comoving frame, the authors derive an exact quantization condition for the spectrum. This condition features an inherently energy-dependent guiding-center parameter, which leads to the identification of physically admissible states and recovers the uniform-field case as a limit. The work extends the Lorentz-covariant approach to nonuniform fields and provides new insights into the role of the guiding center in such systems.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Exact spectrum and anomalous relaxation in the open disorder-free Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev system**|Soshun Ozaki, Hironobu Yoshida, Hosho Katsura|[[arxiv:2606.08079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08079)]|This paper investigates a disorder-free, dissipative version of the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev model using the Lindblad formalism. By exploiting the model's integrability, the authors derive an exact spectrum-resolved solution for the Liouvillian, valid for any system size. A key finding is that the Liouvillian gap, which controls relaxation, does not vanish in the dissipationless limit when the thermodynamic limit is taken first, a phenomenon termed anomalous relaxation. The results reveal complex spectral features and suggest the model is a valuable platform for probing nontrivial open-system quantum dynamics.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Parastatistics in Interacting Periodic Chains Revealed by Peierls Phase Twists and Shifted Conformal Towers**|D. Schuricht, J. In key cases, this reduces to an exactly solvable XXZ chain with flux, and in the gapless regime, the solution exhibits flux-shifted \(c=1\) conformal towers and a temperature-dependent chemical potential. The work provides a concrete method to observe parastatistics in an interacting system and establishes connections to known integrable models.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Full Eigenstate Thermalization in Integrable Spin Systems**|Tanay Pathak|[[arxiv:2510.05887](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05887)]|This paper investigates whether the Eigenstate Thermalization Hypothesis (ETH), extended to higher-order correlations (full ETH), applies to integrable spin systems. They find that, while the fourth-order ETH contribution dominates the late-time dynamics of the out-of-time-ordered correlator (OTOC), its evolution differs significantly from nonintegrable systems. The results highlight the breakdown of standard full ETH assumptions in integrable models, providing insight into how thermalization and its dynamics diverge in these systems.|
|**2026-08-07**|**Odd-parity altermagnetism through sublattice currents: From Haldane-Hubbard model to general bipartite lattices**|Yu-Ping Lin, Marc Vila|[[arxiv:2503.09602](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09602)]|The paper proposes a new mechanism for achieving odd-parity altermagnetism (ALM) using sublattice currents in compensated collinear magnetic systems, which break time-reversal symmetry without net magnetization. Using the Haldane-Hubbard model at half filling as a representative example, the authors show that a ground state previously classified as antiferromagnetic is actually an odd-parity ALM, and its topological version forms an ALM Chern insulator. The results are generalized to common two- and three-dimensional bipartite lattices, indicating that compensated collinear ground states at half filling are generally odd-parity ALMs when sublattice currents are present. This work expands the known classes of altermagnets and provides a new framework for realizing nonrelativistic spin splitting and topological phases in magnetic materials.|
