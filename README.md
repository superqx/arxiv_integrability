|**2026-08-17**|**Towards a Non-Perturbative Classical Double Copy**|Kymani Armstrong-Williams|[[arxiv:2608.14512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14512)]|This paper constructs an exact, non-perturbative solution-generating correspondence between specific ansatz sectors of three theories: quartic biadjoint scalar theory, complexified SU(2) Yang-Mills, and conformally flat gravity. The authors show that a single scalar seed generates factorized solutions in all three theories, with their equations of motion reducing to the same scalar equation, thereby retaining nonlinear dynamics. By additionally enforcing the trace-free part of the Einstein equation, they derive a source relation between gravity and Yang-Mills, leading to a complete classical double copy for these restricted sectors. They illustrate the correspondence with various solutions, including AdS₄ and dS₄, and provide a new example of the Kerr-Schild double copy, demonstrating the dictionary's utility beyond perturbation theory.|

|**2026-08-17**|**Towards a Non-Perturbative Classical Double Copy**|Kymani Armstrong-Williams|[[arxiv:2608.14512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14512)]|This paper establishes a non-perturbative classical double copy by finding exact solution-generating correspondences between restricted ansatz sectors of biadjoint scalar theory, SU(2) Yang-Mills, and gravity. The method shows that a single scalar seed function generates solutions in all three theories, whose equations of motion reduce to the same master equation, with the trace-free Einstein equation yielding an additional source relation. Key results include a dictionary of explicit solutions—such as AdS₄, planar dS₄, and Minkowski space—along with novel Jacobi-elliptic profiles that describe recollapsing or bouncing cosmologies. This work provides the first exact, non-linear double-copy map beyond perturbation theory and offers a new explicit example of the Kerr-Schild double copy.|

This work consolidates and extends existing knowledge, offering a comprehensive and unified framework for these crucial matrix elements in the Sine-Gordon model.|

## Condensed Matter

|Publish Date|Title|Authors|arXiv|Summary|

|**2026-08-17**|**Chiral spin liquid and chiral antiferromagnetism in half-filled moir\'e Hubbard model: possible applications to twisted bilayer TMDs**|Chuyi Tuo, Hong Yao|[[arxiv:2608.14503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14503)]|This paper uses large-scale density matrix renormalization group simulations to study a triangular lattice moiré Hubbard model at half-filling, incorporating a displacement field via a spin-dependent staggered flux. The method reveals that the displacement field dramatically alters the phase diagram, quickly destabilizing the chiral spin liquid, inducing chiral correlations in the 120° antiferromagnetic phase, and stabilizing incommensurate spin-density waves at weaker coupling. A continuous transition between the chiral spin liquid and chiral antiferromagnet is suggested, potentially driven by spinon condensation. The findings provide a roadmap for exploring displacement-field-driven physics in twisted bilayer TMD experiments.|
|**2026-08-17**|**Tuning crystal-fields by He-irradiation and orbital Widom line in SrVO $_3$ films**|Matthias Pickem, Karsten Held, Jan M. Tomczak|[[arxiv:2608.14362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14362)]|This paper re-examines the mechanism behind the helium-ion-irradiation-induced metal-insulator transition in SrVO₃ films. Using DFT plus dynamical mean-field theory, the authors show that the transition is driven by crystal-field splitting from tetragonal expansion rather than a Mott localization from reduced kinetic energy. Their model reproduces experimental spectral gaps and introduces an "orbital Widom line" in the phase diagram, stemming from anomalous compressibility of orbital occupation. The study establishes ion implantation as a viable method to chemically expand correlated materials and highlights orbital, rather than magnetic, ordering at effectively quarter filling.|
|**2026-08-17**|**Anomalous Orbital Reconstruction Controlled by Interorbital Correlations and Hund's Coupling**|Yu Ni, Lvjin Wang, Jinfeng Wang, Yunkun Niu, Zhaoming Fu, Yun Song|[[arxiv:2608.13892](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13892)]|This paper addresses the unresolved origin of anomalous orbital polarization in low-dimensional correlated oxides, where crystal-field effects, electronic correlations, and dimensionality compete. Using dynamical mean-field theory on a two-orbital Hubbard model with orbital-dependent dimensionality, the authors identify interorbital Coulomb interactions as the key mechanism driving orbital redistribution, leading first to an orbital-polarized correlated metal and then to a Mott insulator. They find that Hund's coupling acts as a filling-dependent regulator, weakening this reconstruction at quarter filling but completely suppressing it at half filling by stabilizing balanced high-spin configurations. The results offer a unified framework for correlation-driven orbital phenomena, emphasizing the critical roles of interorbital interactions and Hund's coupling in transition-metal oxides.|
|**2026-08-17**|**Tracking real-space quantum state breathing through Floquet-projector geometry**|Arpit Raj, Johannes Mitscherling, Bj\"orn Trauzettel|[[arxiv:2608.13649](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13649)]|This paper introduces a new theoretical framework, based on "Floquet-projector geometry," for describing quantum states in periodically driven systems. The method unifies the description of stroboscopic evolution and the intra-period "micromotion" by tracking time-evolved projections of quantum states. As a key application, the authors use their formalism to show that the "Floquet-projector quantum metric" directly quantifies the real-space breathing of localized states in a driven spin chain, with topological invariants providing a lower bound on this motion at specific times. The framework offers a systematic tool for linking quantum geometry with dynamic properties and topology in driven lattice systems.|
